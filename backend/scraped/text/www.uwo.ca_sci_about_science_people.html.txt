# Dean's Office Staff

Welcome to the Office of the Dean of Science, the strategic, regulatory, and administrative hub for the Faculty of Science.

**For undergraduate inquiries and questions, including requests for relief and a Dean's Waiver, contact the Academic Dean, Ken Yeung at[aadasci@uwo.ca](mailto:aadasci@uwo.ca).**

For graduate inquiries and questions, contact [gradsci@uwo.ca](mailto:gradsci@uwo.ca).

For faculty and general administrative issues, contact [adfri@uwo.ca](mailto:adfri@uwo.ca).

All other correspondence can be directed to [science@uwo.ca](mailto:science@uwo.ca) and will be forwarded as appropriate.

Note: All undergraduate advising inquiries, including medical accommodations, should be directed to the [ **Academic Advising team**](https://www.uwo.ca/sci/advising/).
