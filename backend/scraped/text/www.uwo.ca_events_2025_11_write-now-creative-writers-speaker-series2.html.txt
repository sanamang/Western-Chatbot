# Write Now! Creative Writers Speaker Series featuring Chido Muchemwa

**Everyone is invited to hear some of Canada’s best established and emerging writers read from their work and discuss their craft.**

Presented by Writing 2520A: Write Now! Writers on Writing, the Department of English and Writing Studies, and the Creative Writers Speaker Series at Western.  
  
**Chido Muchemwa** is a Zimbabwean writer and academic currently based in Toronto. Her short stories have previously appeared in Augur, Baltimore Review, Catapult, FIYAH, Lolwe, and Prism International amongst others. She is a 2022 Miles Morland Scholar, and she has an MFA in Creative Writing from the University of Wyoming. Muchemwa is the author of _Who Will Bury You And Other Stories_ , listed by the _Boston Globe_ , _Open Country Mag_ and _Brittle Paper_ as one of the best books of 2024. Muchemwa is a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of English at the University of Toronto Scarborough.
