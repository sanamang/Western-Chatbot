# Retirees

**Available only to Western's Retired Employees.**

Please email wparking@uwo.ca for information on how to obtain a new retiree parking permit.

*Retirees are issued a complimentary permit which authorizes parking in the [Alumni/Thompson lot](../find/map.html "See Parking Map") at any time (as space permits), any [Honk parking](../find/visitor/metered.html) space on campus (as space permits) and all gated lots after 4 pm and ungated lots after 7 pm Monday to Friday, all day Saturday, Sunday, and Statutory Holidays (posted signage at lot entrances may restrict parking and should be obeyed). **Retiree parking permits are not valid at affiliated colleges.**

A fully refundable hang tag deposit of $30 will be charged at the Parking Office when picking up new permit hang tags. 

If you currently have a retiree permit, you no longer need to renew your permit each year. Your permit will be valid for as long as you would like to have it.

Retiree permits are a benefit to the retiree and can only be used by the retired employee member it is assigned to. Active students and employees are not eligible to use retiree permits. 

*Retirees - Retired members of UWO Faculty may apply for complimentary visitor area parking (depending on space availability).
