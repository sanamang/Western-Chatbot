# Bike Lockers

Located throughout campus, these spacious lockers will provide safety and protection for your bicycle and bike gear. Most standard size bikes will fit, although some tandem bikes or recumbent bikes may not fit into the lockers. 

**Please note: due to safety considerations, electric bikes are permitted in the bike shelters (and lockers) if their battery is UL Certified. Spot checks for compliance are done by Western Fire Safety. If you have any questions about checking your bike for UL Certification, reach out to Parking staff at wparking@uwo.ca.**

If you are interested in renting a bike locker, please send an email to [wparking@uwo.ca](mailto:wparking@uwo.ca) to obtain information on availability.

**Locker Locations:**

  * UCC concrete beach near the Japanese Gardens
  * Behind the Support Services Building (AFAR)
  * Upper Support Services Building
  * Health Sciences Building
  * Amit Chakma Engineering Building (south entrance)
  * Courtyard in between the Natural Sciences Centre and Biological & Geological Sciences Building



**Locker Size:**

3' 6" high, 6' 5" deep, 2' 8" wide

**Rental Information:**

Renters are required to provide a **refundable $100.00 key deposit** , in addition to their rental fees, before they obtain their locker key.

All rental agreements expire on the last day of the arranged rental period at midnight. Renters who wish to renew their contracts must notify the [Parking Office](../about/contact_us.html "Contact Us") of their desire to do so and provide payment at least **2 weeks before** the end of the rental period. Failure to do so may result in renting the locker to another person.

**Rental Rates:**

Rental Period | Cost  
---|---  
4 Months | $40  
8 Months | $80  
12 Months | $100  
**Refund Admin Fee** | **$25**  
  
**Lockouts and Lost Keys:**

Lockout services are only available through Western Special Constables 519-661-3300 (24 hrs/day). A lost key should be reported to the parking office immediately. A replacement key could take up to a full day to process, and an additional $100 deposit will be required.

### Bike Locker Waitlist

The Bike Lockers are offered to the campus community on a first-come, first-served basis. When the available lockers have all been allocated, there will be a waitlist kept. You can email wparking@uwo.ca to indicate you would like to be added to the waitlist. Being on the list will not guarantee space will become available for you to obtain a Bike Locker.

**Secured Bike Shelters:**

Along with the individual bike lockers, Western also offers permits for two secured bike shelters. 

[SEE INFORMATION ABOUT BIKE SHELTERS](secured_bike_shelters.html)

### Protect your bike!

A community-powered bicycle recovery program is connecting riders across the city and alerting the local cycling community if a bike is stolen or goes missing. It's free and easy to register.
