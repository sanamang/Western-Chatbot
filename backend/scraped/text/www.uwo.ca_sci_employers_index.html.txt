# Employers

Western Science prides itself on developing strong, long-lasting relationships with employers while providing quality service that addresses your needs. We promote and administer several employment programs to ensure that you find the best candidate to fill your vacancy.

### 4-16 Month Paid Co-ops

### Science Co-op Program

The Science Co-op Program offers professional, well-trained, and motivated students to meet your recruitment needs. Co-op work terms can be 4 to 16 months for Year 3 students and 4 months (May to August) for Year 2 students. **[See additional program information](coop_program/index.html "Link to information on program") **or feel free to contact [ **Dorota Oliveira** ](mailto:dolive8@uwo.ca "Email Dorota") directly.

### Summer Co-op

### Master of Environment and Sustainability

Western Science's professional **[Master of Environment and Sustainability](https://uwo.ca/mes/employers/index.html "Master of Environment and Sustainability") ** is designed to provide students with the practical skills required for a career in environmental sciences and sustainability. Through a combination of course-based and interdisciplinary learning and a work term, the MES program prepares our students for leadership roles in industry, non-governmental organizations, or in a government department.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact our **[Industry Specialist](mailto:pamela.doxtator@uwo.ca)** directly.

### Master of Data Analytics

**[Master of Data Analytics (MDA)](http://uwo.ca/mda/employers/index.html "Link to MDA website") **students come from quantitative undergraduate degrees (such as statistics, mathematics, computer science, data science, financial modelling, engineering, actuarial science, etc.) and take a deep dive in advanced data science in our one-year professional program. Students can choose to specialize in: 1. Artificial Intelligence; 2. Finance, Banking and Insurance, or 3. The generalist program.

In the third and final term of their studies, students require a 500-hour co-op from May-August of each year, after which they graduate.

Highlights of hiring an MDA student:

  * Skills: Advanced Data Science/AI/ML skills (including statistical modeling and inference; exploratory data analysis; data sorting and cleaning; data munging and wrangling; database management; data visualization; Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and more)
  * Students are seeking summer employment opportunities now, for May/June starts!
  * Students are professionally oriented and able to communicate meaning and insights effectively
  * Students are 100 per cent focused on your needs with zero coursework during the co-op term
  * Students add value and enhance your work, rather than adding to it! The MDA program has intentionally designed the work term to allow employers and supervisors to focus on productivity and mentorship, not excessive grading/evaluations
  * Students are available long-term after August – students graduate August 31 and do not return to studies
  * Flexible to meet your needs – on-site, remote, or hybrid; in London, GTA, or across Canada



Recruiting an MDA student is easy. If you have any questions, feel free to contact our **[Industry Specialist,](mailto:jcuthber@uwo.ca)Juli Bramham, **directly.

### Master of Management of Applied Science

[ **Master of Management of Applied Science** ](https://www.uwo.ca/mmasc/applied_science/index.html "Link to Master of Management of Applied Science website")students come from science or science-related undergraduate degrees (such as medical science, life sciences, computer sciences, social sciences, etc.). The MMASc complements their undergraduate degree with graduate-level study in a curated suite of management studies in our one-year professional program (essentially, a mini MBA).

In the third and final term of their studies, students require a 500-hour co-op from May-August of each year, after which they graduate.

Highlights of hiring an MMASc student:

  * Skills: Project Management, Marketing, Communications, Strategy, Consulting, Organizational Behaviour, Account Management, Finance, Data Driven Decision Making, Business Analytics, and more! MMASc students are traditionally suited to working at the intersection of business and science; however, they are increasingly proving their value in all markets!
  * Students are seeking summer employment opportunities now, for May/June starts!
  * Students are professionally oriented and able to communicate meaning and insights effectively
  * Students are 100 per cent focused on your needs with zero coursework during the co-op term
  * Students add value and enhance your work, rather than adding to it! The MMASc program has intentionally designed the work term to allow employers and supervisors to focus on productivity and mentorship, not excessive grading/evaluations
  * Students are available long-term after August – students graduate August 31 and do not return to studies
  * Flexible to meet your needs – on-site, remote, or hybrid; in London, GTA, or across Canada



Recruiting an MMASc student is easy. If you have any questions, feel free to contact our **[Industry Specialist](mailto:jcuthber@uwo.ca)**, Juli Bramham, directly.
