# Sustainable Transportation

Western University supports environmentally and economically sustainable forms of transportation including:

#### [**Bicycles**](bicycles.html)

There are close to 350 bicycle racks strategically located on campus. A Bike Shelter and lockers are also available for renting.

  * [Learn More](bicycles.html)[](carpooling.html)



#### [**London Transit**](http://www.ltconline.ca/)

Several city buses travel through Western's campus. Schedules and routes can be found on London Transit's website.

  * [Learn More](http://www.ltconline.ca/)



**NEW!** Western offers 37 Electric Vehicle Charging Stations on campus - [LEARN MORE](../../services/ev_charging_stations.html).
