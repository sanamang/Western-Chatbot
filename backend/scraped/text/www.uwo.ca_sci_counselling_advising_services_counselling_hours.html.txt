#### **Live Chat  
**

Talk to an Academic Advising Team Member online. Suitable for quick questions and general inquiries. Live chat will start the last chat 10 minutes prior to end times. Your live chat call may not be taken due to high volume on certain days. If this happens to you, please try again at our next live chat time.

Visit our **[Live Chat](https://www.uwo.ca/sci/advising/advising_services/live_chat.html) ** page for more information. If the live chat bubble is on our page, we are online and ready to chat!

#### **In-Person Same-day Drop-in - NCB 280**

**Monday, Wednesday, Thursday  
**

This is a chance to talk with an advisor in person! A 15 minute advising session to discuss progression, course selection, missed course work, academic issues, and more.

Monday: 10:00 am - 12:00 pm and 1:30-3:30pm

Wednesday: 10:00 am - 12:00 pm -- Welcome Wednesday - First Year Student Priority

Wednesday: 1:30-3:30pm

Thursday: 10:00 am - 12:00 pm

Drop-in ends 10 minutes before 12:00 pm and 3:30 pm – if there are large queues, you may not be served. If this happens, please try again the following day. Stop by NCB 280 to join the queue!

#### **Zoom Same-day Drop-in**

**Tuesday -Thursday: 1:30 pm - 3:30 pm  
**

This is a chance to talk with an advisor live in a Zoom chat one on one. A 15 minute advising session to discuss progression, course selection, missed course work, academic issues, and more.

Drop-in ends 10 minutes before 3:30 pm – if there are large queues or you log in late you may not be served. If this happens, please try again the following day.

**[Zoom appointment link.](https://westernuniversity.zoom.us/s/94485036022) **Passcode: 745451

**Important:** You must use your @uwo.ca email address as your name, or you will not be admitted to the meeting room. Once you get into the waiting room you will be assigned a breakout room for your private conversation. Please be patient during high volumes! We will get to you as soon as possible or send you a message if we are too full.

Not familiar with Zoom? Please see **[Western Technology Services getting started guide](https://wts.uwo.ca/zoom/getting_started.html) **.

#### **In-person Appointments**(will resume after add/drop in September)

**Tuesdays:** 10:00 am-12:00 pm and 1:30-3:30 pm**  
Wednesdays and Thursdays:** 10:00 am - 12:00 pm**  
****Friday:** 10:00 am - 12:00 pm during peak times only (add/drop, exams, etc.)

Appointments are 15 minutes. [](https://help.sci.uwo.ca/servicedesk/customer/portal/3) This is a chance to talk with an advisor face-to-face in our office. Please submit a ticket through our [Academic Advising Help Portal](https://help.sci.uwo.ca/servicedesk/customer/portal/3), selecting the topic that best matches your request. Be sure to include detailed notes indicating that you'd like an appointment, and we'll be happy to assist you. 

#### **TEAMS and Phone Appointments**

Please submit a ticket through our [Academic Advising Help Portal](https://help.sci.uwo.ca/servicedesk/customer/portal/3), selecting the topic that best matches your request. Be sure to include detailed notes indicating that you’d like to book an appointment, and we’ll be happy to assist you.

#### **Welcome Wednesdays - A dedicated drop-in appointment session time for first-year students!**

Science & Basic Medical Sciences Academic Advising office is offering dedicated drop-in hours every Wednesday morning September 10, 2025 – April 29, 2026, to discuss all of your academic related questions.

To compliment your specific discussion points of interest - each month our drop-in sessions will have a theme (e.g. Course Selection, Progression Requirements, Preparing for Exams, Intent to Register etc.) to highlight key upcoming sessional dates of importance, resources available in our office and on campus to support your academic journey.

**Welcome Wednesday Details:**

  * Location: in-person at our office in the North Campus Building, 2nd Floor, Room 280
  * Date: Every Wednesday, September 10, 2025 - April 29, 2026
  * Time: 10:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.



We look forward to meeting with you!
