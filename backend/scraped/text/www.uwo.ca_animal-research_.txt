**Animal Care and Veterinary Services** at Western supplies veterinarians, animal care technicians, managers and office staff to service and facilitate Western's research community throughout London in ethically-sound and ground-breaking research using animals.

ACVS is dedicated to moving safe and ethical animal research forward for Western and its affiliates by offering the following services:

  * [Managing three on-campus facilities](facilities/index.html "ACVS-Managed Facilities")
  * [Animal Use Protocol processing for the ACC](https://uwo.ca/research/ethics/animal/ "Animal Use Protocols at Western")
  * [Mandated Animal User Training](acc-services/research_education/index.html "Research Education at Western")
  * [Institutional-and-affiliate-wide research support](research/index.html "Research Support at Western")
  * [Facilitation and Support for Western's Animal Research Database Management Software, eSirius](esirius/index.html "eSirius")
