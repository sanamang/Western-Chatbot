# Orientation & Onboarding

Welcome to the Faculty of Health Sciences!

As you begin this next stage of your career, we want to make your transition easy. This website is here to assist you in orienting you to the Faculty of Health Sciences at Western University.

**Items to Complete**

  * **[Western Identity/Email](https://wts.uwo.ca/identity/identities_and_access/identities_new_student.html)** \- To activiate your email / Obtain a Western ID. If you encounter issues you can connect with [WTS Help Desk](https://wts.uwo.ca/helpdesk/index.html)
  * [**WesternOne (ID) Card**](https://registrar.uwo.ca/services/facultystaff.html) \- Used for building/room access and can be used to pay for food at on-campus eateries
  * [**Direct Deposit**](http://www.uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/pay_admin/doc/critical_info.pdf) \- To be set up in payroll for monthly deposits
  * [**Parking Permit**](https://www.uwo.ca/parking/) \- If you plan on driving to campus, you can purchase a parking permit here
  * [**Keys**](https://www.uwo.ca/fm/client_services/keys.html) \- Request keys to your office or other key access rooms
  * [**Voice Mail**](https://wts.uwo.ca/telecom/voicemail/voicemail_to_email_service.html) \- To set up your office voicemail or voice to email
  * [**Health & Safety Training**](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/learning/required/index.html) \- Required Training and Training based on role can be accessed here
