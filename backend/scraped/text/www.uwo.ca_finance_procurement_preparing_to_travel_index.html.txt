# Preparing to Travel

Before traveling on Western business, learn more about travel policies & procedures, booking travel and expense reimbursement with this (~6min) video. [Travel Booking and Expense Claims - Micro Module](../../restricted/training_reference_content/procurement/travel_expense_module/story.html "Travel Booking and Expense Claims - Micro Module")

Full details about travel services can be found [here](../../restricted/preparing_to_travel.html "Click here for travel details").

#### Travel Agency - Direct Travel

Direct Travel is a global travel management company that focuses on providing personalized services for business travel. Our partnership with Direct Travel has provided us with access to *online booking tool, which will allow Western travelers to book airfare, hotel accommodations, and car rentals through the online tool. 

For Direct Travel details and contact information [click here](../../restricted/preparing_to_travel.html "Direct Travel contact information")

**_* For access to the online booking tool email_**[** _procurement@uwo.ca_**](mailto:procurement@uwo.ca) _  
_

#### Hotel Rates

Procurement Services has negotiated rates for Western at local hotels for guests visiting Western. We have also provided the CAUBO hotel rates for across Canada which faculty and staff can use as a CAUBO member. 

To access the full information about available hotels and rates [click here](../../restricted/preparing_to_travel.html "Preparing to Travel - Details").

#### Train Travel

Discounted prices are available from VIA RAIL for all Western faculty and staff. VIA RAIL offers a 15% discount off of the best available public fare (subject to advance purchase requirements) and applies to most train fare classes and routes (excludes Escape Fares). This rate does not apply to multi-trip pass products and any promotional fare requiring a discount code such as: Canrailpass, BizPak, Corridorpass, 6-Pak, and GO/VIA Pak.   
This rate applies to both business and leisure train trips taken by Western faculty and staff and will be extended to up to three (3) additional passengers. Tickets must be purchased from VIA directly or through Western's preferred Travel Agencies. [Book here.](../../restricted/preparing_to_travel.html "Preparing to Travel - Details")

#### Accident Information

Report any accident to the University's Insurance Office as soon as possible at 661-2111 extension 85631 or email the [Corporate Insurance Administrator](mailto:hrhelp@uwo.ca) _._ If a Western VISA card was charged and the vehicle was damaged, please report the accident to the Scotiabank VISA customer service office (telephone 1-888-823-9657).

The following information will be collected from Western's insurance department:

  1.      1.         1. Faculty/Department
        2. Date of Incident
        3. Time of Incident
        4. Vehicle Make, Year and Serial Number
        5. Driver's Name and Phone Number
        6. Accident Location
        7. Other Parties' Names and Phone Numbers
        8. Officer's Name if Campus/City Police Called
        9. Brief Description of Accident

#### Top 4 Travel Questions

  1. **Does Western have a preferred travel management company?**   
Direct Travel can be used to make travel arrangements. Refer to the “Travel Agency” section for more information.
  2. **How do I book travel online?  
** Procurement Services must set you up with a travel account before you can begin booking travel. Contact us at [procurement@uwo.ca](mailto:procurement@uwo.ca) to set up an account.
  3. **My spouse/partner is traveling with me, how can I make arrangements for him/her?**   
Arrangements for spouses or partners must be made through a Direct Travel Manager. These arrangements cannot be made through the online booking tool.
  4. **How can I pay for travel?**
     * Most travel expenses are paid for personally upfront and then reimbursed through an expense claim.
     * Plane and train tickets can be charged directly to your speed code through Direct Travel, online or through an agent.
     * The **Western Travel Card** , through Scotiabank, can be used to pay for travel upfront. The cardholder must submit supporting documentation for reimbursement of expenses charged to the Travel Card and make payment within the terms of the card to avoid late charges. Note: This is different than the Scotiabank Pcard. Email [procurement@uwo.ca](mailto:procurement@uwo.ca) for more information.
     * A _Travel Coordinator role_ can be added to your Scotiabank Pcard to charge hospitality for group events and to book travel for non-University employees.
