# The Western Centre for Brain and Mind

### A place for Interdisciplinary Research.   
Western Centre for Brain and Mind brings together researchers from across disciplines,  
offering support for collaborations with cutting-edge shared resources to tackle   
important questions in cognitive neuroscience.

* * *

# [The Brain and Mind Core Facility](research/research_facility/index.html)

# 

### The Brain and Mind Core Facility is housed in the Western Interdisciplinary Research Building.   
It is a multidisciplinary facility supporting collaborative approaches to research   
with access to new technologies and innovative methods of measuring brain activity and participant behaviour.

* * *

# [Participants](research/participants.html)

# 

### If you are interested in participating in the research taking place at the Centre for Brain and Mind,   
become a member of Western's Cognitive Neuroscience Research Registry - [OurBrainsCAN.](https://ourbrainscan.uwo.ca/sign_up.html)  
  
The OurBrainsCAN [website](https://ourbrainscan.uwo.ca/about/index.html#WhatIsOurBrainsCAN) provides details about the registry, who uses the it, and current studies.   
Email question to [ourbrainscan@uwo.ca](mailto:ourbrainscan@uwo.ca)
