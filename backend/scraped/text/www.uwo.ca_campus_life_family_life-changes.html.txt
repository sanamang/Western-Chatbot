# Life Changes

In This Section

## Information for Faculty:

  


### Caregiving

Below you will find several government and public sector resources providing information on senior and adult care to properly address your family's specific needs and the options and alternatives available to address those concerns.

#### General Resources

  * [Seniors' Info](http://www.health.gov.on.ca/en/public/programs/ltc/default.aspx) \- Informational web resources for seniors and their families
  * [Ontario Senior's Secretariat](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-seniors-accessibility) \- Informational web resource for programs for seniors and caregivers in Ontario

  
  


### Marriage

#### Western Resources

  * If you are changing your address you may make that change online in [My Human Resources](https://uwo.ca/hr/my_hr/index.html), or you may complete a [Personal Data Form](https://uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/pay_admin/form/pdf.pdf).
  * If you are changing your name, please download and complete a [Personal Data Form](https://uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/pay_admin/form/pdf.pdf) and mail or fax it to us at the address on the form. You may also scan and email it to [hrhelp@uwo.ca](mailto:hrhelp@uwo.ca). You will need to attach copies of two pieces of identification to support the change, including one piece of photo identification.
  * You may wish to add your new spouse to your benefits coverage and review your beneficiary designations. To do so, please complete the [Benefit Application/Change form](https://uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/benefits/form/benefit_change.pdf). **Note:** when adding/increasing life insurance, a medical questionnaire may be required if more than 31 days have passed since the first day of your eligibility or your spouse/dependent’s eligibility for the insurance. You may wish to contact [Human Resources](https://uwo.ca/hr/contact.html) for assistance in understanding your current coverage and the options available to you. To review your benefits visit the [Your Benefits](https://uwo.ca/hr/benefits/your_benefits/index.html) website and choose your employee group.

  
  


#### Community Resources

  * [Information](https://www.ontario.ca/page/getting-married) about getting married from the Province of Ontario.
  * Obtaining a Marriage License 
    * [Ministry of Ontario](https://www.forms.ssb.gov.on.ca/mbs/ssb/forms/ssbforms.nsf/AttachDocsPublish/007-11018E~1/$File/11018E.pdf)
    * [City of London](https://london.ca/living-london/community-services/marriage-oaths)

  
  


### New Baby

#### Western Resources

  * Western's Human Resources has developed a [Prenatal, Pregnancy and Adoption page](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/benefits/leave/index.html) that outlines the processes of applying for a leave after the arrival of your baby. The [Life Events website](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/life-events.html) through Service Canada offers detailed resources on how to register for your baby's birth certificate, social insurance number, OHIP, and passport.
  * You may add your child to your Extended Health and Dental plan using the [Benefit Application/Change form](https://uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/benefits/form/benefit_change.pdf).
  * You may wish to add or increase life insurance coverage and review your current life and pension beneficiary designations. Contact [Human Resources](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/contact.html) for assistance reviewing your current coverage and coverage options available to you.

  
  


#### Community Resources

  * Telehealth Ontario: 1-866-797-0000. Free access to a registered nurse 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  * [Middlesex County Health Unit](https://www.healthunit.com/) \- Provides information on breastfeeding, weight clinics, and immunizations.
  * [Ontario Early Years](https://www.ontario.ca/page/provide-child-care-and-early-years-programs) \- Essential resources on preparing for the arrival of baby.
  * [Childreach](https://childreach.on.ca/) \- London's Ontario Years Centre - Contains information on playgroups, structured programs, and parenting information.

  
  


### Separation and Divorce

#### Western Resources

  * Western's Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers confidential, face-to-face and secure web-based counselling, 24/365 emergency telephone counselling, telephone advisory services, wellness workshops, and psychoeducational groups. The EAP is available through the facilities of FSEAP Thames Valley / Family Service Thames Valley.   
  
[FSEAP Thames Valley ](https://fstv.ca/)  
125 Woodward Ave.   
London, Ontario N6H 2H1   
Tel: 519-433-0700   
Fax: 519-433-4273   
  
Hours of Operation   
Monday - Thursday: 9:00am - 8:00pm   
Friday: 9:00am - 5:00pm   
After hours, call: 1-800-668-9920   
  
If you are changing your address you may make that change online in [My Human Resources](https://uwo.ca/hr/my_hr/index.html), or you may complete a personal Data Form.   
  
If you are changing your name, please download and complete a [Personal Data Form](https://uwo.ca/hr/form_doc/pay_admin/form/pdf.pdf) and mail or fax it to us at the address on the form. You may also scan and email it to [hrhelp@uwo.ca](mailto:hrhelp@uwo.ca). You will need to attach copies of two pieces of identification to support the change, including one piece of photo identification.   
  
You will be required to remove your spouse from your benefits if they no longer meet the definition of a spouse. You may also wish to add or increase life insurance coverage and/or change one or more of your beneficiaries. Contact [Human Resources](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/contact.html) for assistance reviewing your current coverage and coverage options available to you.

  
  


#### Community Resources

  * [Divorce and Separation Ontario](https://www.attorneygeneral.jus.gov.on.ca/english/family/divorce/index.php) \- A resource offered by the province of Ontario that outlines the legal issues and the process of obtaining a divorce in Ontario.
  * [Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/) \- Divorce Law Questions and Answers on the process of divorce, financial matters, and children.
  * [Supporting Families](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-justice/news/2016/07/supporting-families-experiencing-separation-or-divorce.html) \- Information from the Department of Justice on family issues, separation, and divorce
  * [Ministry of Attorney General](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-attorney-general) \- Information to better understand the legalities of divorce in Ontario and its impact on family members
  * [Legal Aid Ontario](https://www.legalaid.on.ca/en/)
