## Book a Personal Consultation

A personal consultation is the best way to have your questions answered. All consultants are salaried employees of Sun Life which helps ensure _your goals_ are the _only goals_. [Learn more about navigating support throughout your journey.](../form_doc/pension/doc/presentations/navigating_support.pdf)

### 2/Meet with a Financial Services Consultant by phone or Zoom, year-round

A Financial Services Consultant with Sun Life's Plan Advice service can provide you with personal, unbiased, one-on-one investment advice on your Western Pension Plan AND they can also help you build your broader _**One Plan**_ financial plan. Book an appointment between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., and if you wish, include a spouse or trusted friend in your planning discussion. Once connected, you’ll be able to reach out to your consultant when you have questions as your plan evolves.

### 3/ Meet with a Sun Life Retirement Consultant - by phone, video or in-person, year-round

Retirement Consultants provide dedicated support to members who are within five to seven years of retiring. If you decide to stay with Sun Life in retirement, you’ll keep working with your consultant.

## Go online!

You have 24/7 online and mobile access at [mysunlife.ca/western](https://www.sunlife.ca/western) to view your account balances, make investment changes and access tools.

## New to Sun Life online? Here's what you need to know.

The first time you access your account at Sun Life you must register. Instructions for registration may be found below. Once you have registered you may login:

  * Active employees may use their Western user ID and password on the [Pension Plan login page at Western](login.html).
  * All members can go directly to [mysunlife.ca/western](https://www.sunlife.ca/western) and use their Sun Life access ID and password to login.*****
  * Members may download the my Sun Life Mobile App, available for smart phones to review their account online, any time, on the go*****
  * Your account number is 03479 followed by your 9-digit Western ID number.



*** Note** – you may be asked to authenticate and provide further information at login.

Need assistance? Please contact the Sun Life Customer Care Centre at 1-866-733-8612.

### Registering for access to your Sun Life account

The first time you login to your account, either through the Western login page (using your Western credentials) or directly at [mysunlife.ca/western](https://www.sunlife.ca/western), you will be asked to verify your identity and register. Here is information you may be required to provide:

  * Your Western employee ID (nine digits) or your Sun Life account number (03479 followed by your 9-digit Western ID number)
  * Your date of birth, country of residence, postal code and your account number
  * You will be prompted to establish a Sun Life access ID and password (unless you have done so for a previous Sun Life account). These will be used anytime you log directly into [mysunlife.ca/western](https://www.sunlife.ca/western) or use the my Sun Life Mobile App.



If you have any questions or require help to register, please contact the Sun Life Customer Care Centre at 1-866-733-8612 any business day between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.
