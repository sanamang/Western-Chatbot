# International Funding Opportunities

Western is in many respects, an international university. Many staff and faculty have come from or received training, abroad. By expanding our horizons to the world, we are able to offer students an expanded array of educational experiences that will prepare them for the global economy. Internationalization also enables us to expand research programs for broader impact and to more effectively help those in need in developing countries and areas of conflict.
