# Faculty (UWOFA)

For all **Limited Duties** postings (excluding course authoring appointments), please visit [**Working at Western, Employment Opportunities.**](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/working/staff/ "Opens in new window") Working at Western opportunities have expanded to include online applications for Limited Duties appointments, moving a highly paper-based, slower process to an efficient, secure online experience.

When at the site, please select the appropriate link under the **Logging in** section. From the Working at Western Career’s Home page, **View all Jobs** and search by **Posting Type:** **Faculty** and use the other search features to find the Department/School/Faculty you wish to explore for opportunities.

**Please note: Some of the PDF's are fillable and your web browser may not be able to display them. To use these forms:**

  1.      1. Right-click on the link.
     2. In the menu that appears, click on "Save link as..." and save the file to your computer.
     3. Open up Adobe Reader (or similar PDF reader).
     4. Within Adobe Reader, click on the "File" menu on the top and choose "Open".
     5. Locate the file you just saved, click on it to choose it, and then click on the "Open" button.
