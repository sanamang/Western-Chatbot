# Alumni

****

**Western Science Alumni – Let's Connect!**  
  
As a member of the Western Science alumni community, you are part of a global network. Your achievements continue to inspire current students and strengthen the Western Science brand locally, nationally, and internationally.  
  
We’re excited to explore meaningful ways for you to engage with the Faculty of Science – whether through philanthropy, mentorship, hiring students for co-op opportunities, or other avenues. Together, we can help shape the future of science and create transformative experiences for the next generation of innovators.  
  
If you’re interested in learning more about how to get involved, please connect with our Development team:  
  
**Karmen Ferraro**  
Senior Development Officer  
[karmen.ferraro@uwo.ca](mailto:karmen.ferraro@uwo.ca)

Looking to hire a student for a Summer or Long-Term Co-op, or hire a new graduate? Reach out to Dorota Oliveira from Science Careers ([dolive8@uwo.ca](mailto:dolive8@uwo.ca)) to discuss your recruitment needs and discover ways to engage with our students.  
  
Together, we can make a lasting impact on the next generation of scientists, innovators, and leaders. We look forward to connecting with you!
