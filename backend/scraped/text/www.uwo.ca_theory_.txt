## **[News& Events](events/index.html)**

**[Admission Applications Now Open!](future_students/Admission.html)** Applications open October 1 and the deadline to apply is January 8, 2026. [](future_students/Admission.html)

* * *

[Nominations and applications are invited](people/Director-Advertisement---final-2025.pdf) for the position of **Director of the Centre for the Study of Theory and Criticism** at Western University.

* * *

[Theory Sessions presents](events/theory_sessions.html): **The State, The People, Pueblo, and Two Populisms.** Friday, November 21. 

* * *

[**The Royal Society of Canada**](https://rsc-src.ca/sites/default/files/25_New%20Members_EN-combined.pdf#page=33) recognized seven Western scholars for their outstanding contributions to the arts, humanities and sciences, including our very own **Dr. Joel Faflak**. 

* * *

**Recent books by alumni:** Andrew Woods has a book forthcoming called "The Cultural Marxism Conspiracy:  _Why the Right Blames the Frankfurt School for the Decline of the West_." [See more here.](https://www.versobooks.com/en-ca/products/3239-the-cultural-marxism-conspiracy?srsltid=AfmBOoqj2-uRZrSa7em3AushcjeV3yNEdCA4VF3sALkcN-PZwgddjqad)

* * *

**New collaborative specialization!** The Theory Centre has become a member of the [Transitional Justice collaborative graduate specialization](https://tjcentre.uwo.ca), and Theory students are now able to add this on to their program and degree. For information on the requirements and benefits, see [tjcentre.uwo.ca](https://tjcentre.uwo.ca). 

* * *

Current student Brendan Brown has published two papers, "[A Democracy (Never) to Come: Fugitivity and Metaphysics in Afropessimism](https://scholarlypublishingcollective.org/psup/speculative-philosophy/article-abstract/39/3/252/401618/A-Democracy-Never-to-Come-Fugitivity-and?redirectedFrom=fulltext)" and "[Anarcho-Pacifism: Derrida, Benjamin, The General Strike and its Afterlife in Arnold Roller’s Der Generalstreik und die Soziale Revolution](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0969725X.2025.2529732)."
