# Affiliate Parking

  


* * *

#### [**Huron University College**](https://huronu.ca/parking/)

Parking is available to purchase in the "Student/Visitor" lot for all Huron University College students on a first come/first served basis beginning in June. Permits are valid from September 1 to August 31 annually.

* * *

#### [**Kings University College**](http://www.kings.uwo.ca/current-students/money-matters/parking-transportation/parking-services/)

There are [six parking lots](http://www.kings.uwo.ca/kings/assets/File/maps/CampusSite.pdf "See Map") on the King’s University College Campus. A limited number of guaranteed parking spots are available for purchase by students. Please make inquiries through the [Student Financial Services Office](http://www.kings.uwo.ca/about-kings/who-we-are/administrative-departments/finance/student-financial-services/).
