# Visitor Parking Payments

**Accepted Forms of payment at Western Parking gates – Amex, MasterCard and Visa.**

**DEBIT/TAP IS NOT ACCEPTED**

Customers utilizing visitor parking spaces are expected to have an acceptable method of payment. Customers wishing to pay by coin should use the Pay and Display machines located across campus. See the [**PARKING MAP**](../map.html) for Pay and Display locations.

When payment cannot be made at the gate, a Customer Service Representative may take customer information so that a fee can be collected later and the parker can be on their way. While making payment later may be convenient for customers, our parking gate system is not set up to efficiently and effectively take payments/information over the intercom; for this reason, an administrative fee is charged on all non-payment transactions.

**When you have failed to make payment at the time of parking:**

  * it is the customer’s responsibility to reach out to the Parking Office if needed, and to clear up outstanding parking fees
  * gate parking charges are not eligible for appeal

**How to pay your visitor parking charge:**

Following your parking transaction, log into the Parking Services Payment Portal (below) to make the payment online with a credit card. _Please note that it may take up to two business days for your gate fee to show up on your Parking Account._ Alternatively, you can visit the Parking Office and make payment in person at the service counter.

[**PARKING PAYMENT PORTAL**](https://westernu.t2hosted.ca/Account/Portal)

**Tips for paying your gate charge online:**

  * To find any outstanding parking fees, enter your vehicle plate information (no spaces) in the "Plate Number" box and click “search”
  * All charges and citations associated with the vehicle plate will be displayed
  * Add fees to be paid to your “basket” and make payment with a credit card
