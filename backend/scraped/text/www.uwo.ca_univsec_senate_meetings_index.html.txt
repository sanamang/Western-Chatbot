# Meetings of the Senate

Senate meetings are held on **Fridays at 1:30 p.m. in the Great Hall, Somerville House,** unless otherwise noted.

The Agenda is usually posted a week in advance of the meeting on the [**Agendas and Minutes webpage**](agendas_minutes.html).

**2025-26 Senate Meeting Dates:**

  * September 19, 2025
  * October 17, 2025
  * November 14, 2025
  * December 5, 2025
  * January 16, 2026
  * February 13, 2026
  * March 13, 2026
  * April 17, 2026
  * May 15, 2026
  * June 5, 2026
