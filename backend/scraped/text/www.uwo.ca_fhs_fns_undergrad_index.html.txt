# Undergraduate Programs

### Looking to positively impact communities through nutrition and human development?

The Brescia School of Food and Nutritional Sciences is home to two undergraduate programs:  Foods & Nutrition and Family Studies & Human Development . Both programs are designed to provide a comprehensive education with opportunities for hands-on experiences.

## Foods   
& Nutrition

## Foods & Nutrition: Future Student Webinar

[ Watch Now ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Omvs7rDMV0U)

Foods & Nutrition provides a comprehensive education in nutrition through multidisciplinary perspectives like societal health determinants and the impact of culture on food habits.

### Program Options

    * [Foods & Nutrition](foods-nutrition.html)
    * [Foods & Nutrition Chemical Engineering Dual Degree](foods-nutrition-and-chemical-engineering-dual-degree.html)
    * [Nutrition & Dietetics](nutrition-dietetics.html)



## Family Studies   
& Human Development

## Family Studies & Human Development: Future Student Webinar

[ Watch Now ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-SfL24K5c)

Explore and understand the complexities and challenges of human development and family structure through a critical socioeconomic lens, with the opportunity to receive the Professional Home Economist designation (PHEc) with the Ontario Home Economics Association—and make a positive impact on real families in the community.
