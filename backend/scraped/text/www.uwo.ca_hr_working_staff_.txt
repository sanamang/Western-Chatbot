# Employment Opportunities

## Welcome!

Founded in 1878, Western delivers an exemplary learning experience that engages the best and brightest people. Those who work at our University have the opportunity to pursue a career that is rewarding, engaging and satisfying. We encourage you to consider a career at Western and become a part of ['The Western Experience](https://www.uwo.ca/about/whoweare/index.html "Western Who We Are")'.

## What positions are posted on Working at Western?

On**Working at Western** you will find:

  * Staff positions (part-time and full-time)
  * Faculty positions (part-time)



Full-time faculty positions can be accessed from the menu on the left. New opportunities arise frequently so please check back often. There may be more than one page of job postings.

## Use "Search Jobs" to find positions

Use the **Search Jobs** bar at the top of **Careers** to quickly search for jobs. Type in a keyword and click the arrow at right.

On the left panel of **Search Jobs** , use the filter to select which jobs display in the main panel. For example, under **Posting Type** , click **Faculty** to display only faculty postings. Under **Faculty/Unit** , select the faculty or unit you wish to explore.

## Submitting your application

When you apply online for a position for the first time, you will be asked to provide an active email address and current phone number, as well as details about your employment history and qualifications.

You have the option to upload attachments such as a cover letter or resume along with your application. Learn more about this and find other helpful information in our updated guide [Applying to Working at Western](../../my_hr/guides_open/apply_to_work/index.html).

**Western Inclusion, Diversity and Equity (WIDE) Survey -** Western now collects applicant equity information. [The WIDE Survey](https://www.edi.uwo.ca/initiatives/wide-survey/) is available during the application process is voluntary and will help inform, assess, and diversify Western’s recruitment and talent acquisition strategies and policies. The data will always be reported in aggregate form only to hiring units to maintain the anonymity of respondents.

## Logging in

If you are experiencing difficulty with a specific browser please try a different browser or [read our troubleshooting tips](http://uwo.ca/hr/working/staff/troubleshooting.html).

To go to **Working at Western** , select the link that best describes you: **  
**

[I am a current Western employee or I have worked at Western within the past six months](https://myhr.uwo.ca/psp/hrprdweb/?cmd=login)

The above link will allow you to log into My Human Resources. Select **Working at Western** to bring up **Careers** , where you may review postings and apply for jobs.

[User name and password assistance for current and recent employees](../../my_hr/how_do_i.html)

[I do not work at Western and have not worked at Western within the past six months](https://recruit.uwo.ca)

The above link will take external applicants to **Careers** , where you may review postings and apply for jobs. First-time external applicants will select **New User** or **Register Now** and create a username and password. Before registering, you must acknowledge that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.  
  
If you have applied before, select **Sign In** and log in with the username and password you previously created. Select **Forgot User Name** or **Forgot Password** if you need assistance.

After login, select **My Account Information** to add or update your personal and contact information.

### **Working At Western weekly email**

[Subscribe to our mailing list](https://forms.office.com/r/0a9aM59c9G) to stay informed on the newest staff job postings on Working at Western and never miss an opportunity!

Once subscribed, you will receive an email every Wednesday with staff jobs that have been posted within the last week. Please note, this is a confidential mailing list only available for Western employees and your information will not be shared.

## Western is committed to employment equity

Western is committed to employment equity and diversity in the workplace and welcomes applications from women, members of racialized groups, Indigenous persons, persons with disabilities, persons of any sexual orientation, and persons of any gender identity or gender expression. Western is dedicated to ensuring that each individual will have genuine, open and unhindered access to employment opportunities.

[Learn more about diversity in the workplace](../../diversity/index.html)

Accommodations are available for applicants with disabilities throughout the recruitment process. If you require accommodations for interviews or other meetings, please [contact Human Resources](../../contact.html).
