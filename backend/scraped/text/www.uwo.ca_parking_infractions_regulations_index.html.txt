# Parking Regulations

Remember that parking lots at the University are private property. Purchase of any parking permit or day pass constitutes an agreement from the purchaser to abide to the rules and regulations [](/infractions/pdf/rules_regulations.pdf)approved for the University community by the Board of Governors.

[1.37 Parking and Traffic Regulations](https://www.uwo.ca/univsec/pdf/policies_procedures/section1/mapp137.pdf)

  * [Procedures](https://www.uwo.ca/univsec/pdf/policies_procedures/section1/mapp137_procedure.pdf)

  


### [Parking Infractions](parking_infractions.html)

  * Issued by [Western Special Constable Service](http://www.uwo.ca/police/) as well as trained Parking Enforcement Staff



### [Traffic Infractions](traffic_infractions.html)

  * Issued by [Western Special Constable Service](http://www.uwo.ca/police/)



### Please do not ignore your parking ticket!

Accounts with unpaid fines run the risk of the following actions:

  * Parking privileges revoked
  * Academic records sealed
  * Vehicles towed
  * Account balance transfered to a collection agency
