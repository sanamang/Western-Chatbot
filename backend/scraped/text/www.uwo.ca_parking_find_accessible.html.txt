# Accessible Parking on Campus

### Accessible Western Parking Permit

Members of the University Community with long term or temporary physical disabilities may apply for access to all areas designated with the recognized international symbol. [Rates ](../permits/accessible.html) for an Accessible Western Parking Permit are equivalent to the Perimeter Non-Reserved fees specific to your status on campus. If you require a Western Accessible Permit, please send an email to [wparking@uwo.ca](mailto:wparking@uwo.ca and we'll) and we'll get you set up with the appropriate permit. [](mailto:wparking@uwo.ca and we'll)

### Where can I park if I don't have an Accessible Western Parking Permit?

Visitors who require accessible parking are granted access to all [Pay and Display and Honk Mobile zones](visitor/metered.html) on campus. Please note, parking must be paid when displaying an [Ontario Ministry of Transportation Accessible Parking Permit](http://www.ontario.ca/Accessible%20parking%20permit).

### Accessibility Map

Western’s [Campus Accessibility Map](https://accessibility.uwo.ca/doc/floorplan/campus.pdf) may be of assistance in locating accessible routes between buildings on campus.
