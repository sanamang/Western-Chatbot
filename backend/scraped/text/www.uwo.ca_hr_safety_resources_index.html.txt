# Resources & Reports

There are many resources, including important manuals, procedures and guidelines available to assist you in creating a safe work place. **The menu on your left** offers links to these resources, as well as to some external resources that may also be of interest.

**The Health & Safety Bulletin Board** (below) provides links to many of the same reports and resources you might find on the bulletin board in your faculty or department.

If you require Human Resources forms, documents or other information in an alternative format, please [use **ASK HR** to contact Human Resources](../../contact.html).

## Health & Safety Bulletin Board

### ***NEW*** \- Safety Abroad

Western International offers a [helpful step-by-step guide](https://international.uwo.ca/learning/safety_abroad/steps_for_faculty_staff.html) on their website for faculty and staff who are travelling internationally. The site also includes FAQs, as well as helpful government and health unit resources.

### Occupational Health & Safety Act - Ontario

Ontario's [**Occupational Health and Safety Act**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90o01) sets out the rights and duties of all parties in the workplace, as well as the procedures for dealing with workplace hazards and for enforcement as needed.

You may also be interested in reading the **[Guide to the Occupational Health and Safety Act](https://www.ontario.ca/document/guide-occupational-health-and-safety-act)**.
