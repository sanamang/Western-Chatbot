# Student Life

Arts & Humanities Students' Council

The Arts & Humanities Students' Council are a dynamic and passionate group of students who enhance student life through academic and social events, publications, exhibits & more!

[More](http://www.ahscwesternu.com/ "blue-box example")

Student Clubs

Get involved in our many student clubs and share your passion for visual art, philosophy, classical studies, film & more.

. [More](http://www.uwo.ca/arts/current_students/undergraduate/clubs_groups.html "blue-box example")

Publish Your Work

Passion for writing? Publish your work in one of the Students' Council's annual creative and academic journals, or write for Occasus the literary journal in Writing Studies.

[More](http://www.ahscwesternu.com/about-our-pubs "blue-box example")

Study Abroad

Practice your language skills in Italy or Spain! Or experience the culture of Germany, Rwanda, or Cuba - there are opportunities to study just about anywhere around the world!

[More](http://www.uwo.ca/arts/current_students/undergraduate/study_abroad.html "blue-box example")

Impact Experience

Join other Western students and staff participating in a hands-on service-learning experience offered throughout the year in a variety of destinations such as London, Costa Rica, Italy, Guatemala and more!

[More](https://cel.uwo.ca/ "blue-box example")

Western Student Film Festival

Each year the Western Undergraduate Film Society organizes a student film festival with a screening event at Wolf Performance Hall.

[More](https://www.facebook.com/WesternUndergraduateFilmSociety/?eid=ARBZlBiJdfF32axV60rlYWOciw38WzE5o8qxRy7QA2sI0PlaXt-eqoLdMSeYR72Nm3PSwPZC519LiWIb "blue-box example")

Internships

Arts and Humanities Internship Program (AHIP) provides undergraduate students with the opportunity to develop the skills they're learning in their program, build their resume and professional network, and explore career interests.

[More](https://www.uwo.ca/arts/internships/index.html "blue-box example")

Student Writer-in-Residence

Western's SWIR fosters exchange among aspiring student writers and contributes to the culture of creativity across campus, within the London area, and across literary genres, media, and technologies.

[More](http://www.uwo.ca/english/people/student-in-res.html "blue-box example")

Theatre Productions

Get involved on stage or behind the scenes! Our Theatre Studies and French Studies programs offer course credit for participating as cast and crew in community productions

[More](http://www.uwo.ca/theatrestudies/ "blue-box example")
