# Admissions

### 

### Admission requirements:

Admission requirements vary based on where you are applying from. For specific details about admission requirements visit the [Welcome Website](https://welcome.uwo.ca/next-steps/requirements/index.html).

* * *

### How to apply:

For details about how to apply to Western as well as information on scholarships, residence, student life and much more, visit the[ Welcome Website.](https://welcome.uwo.ca/next-steps/requirements/index.html)

For details on how to apply to the **School for Advanced Studies in the Arts & Humanities**, visit the [SASAH website.](https://www.uwo.ca/arts/sasah/program/requirements.html)

For details on how to apply to the **BFA program** , visit the [Department of Visual Arts website](http://www.uwo.ca/visarts/undergraduate/bfa/apply.html).

* * *
