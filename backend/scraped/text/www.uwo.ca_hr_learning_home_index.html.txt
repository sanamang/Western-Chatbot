# Working Virtually

Workshops and resources to support employees working virtually on campus, or remotely.

#### **Software Resources**

### Microsoft Outlook Level 200

If you are new to Outlook, you may want to review the Microsoft Outlook "Get Started Guide" before watching this workshop: [Microsoft Outlook Office Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook?ui=en-us&rs=en-us&ad=us)

**Watch a Recording of the Workshop (90 Minutes):** Facilitated by Miguel Mojica (Training Team, Microsoft), this workshop is for Outlook users who have a good working knowledge of the basics and are wanting to learn some of the more advanced features. Topics covered include: creating inbox rules and organization, delayed delivery, mail recall, calendar sharing and the scheduling assistant tool.

Click [here](https://aka.ms/A-UWO-Teams200-Aug9) to watch a recording of a previous session.

### Microsoft Teams Level 100

[Microsoft Teams 101 Quick Guide](../../form_doc/learning/microsoft_teams_101_quick_guide.pdf) (PDF Document)

For help on using teams, please visit the [WTS Microsoft Teams Learning page](https://wts.uwo.ca/microsoft_teams/index.html).

**Watch a Recording of the Workshop (90 Minutes):** Facilitated by Michael Chin (Customer Success Trainer, Microsoft), this session provides an introduction to Microsoft Teams and reviews the basic features needed to start working in one’s first Team.

To view the recording:

  * Go to the WTS [Microsoft Teams](https://wts.uwo.ca/microsoft_teams/index.html) website
  * Select “ _Microsoft Teams Level 100 Training Session - March 2021_ ” 



### Microsoft Teams Level 200

**Watch a Recording of the Workshop (90 Minutes):** Facilitated by Miguel Mojica (Training Team, Microsoft), this workshop is designed for someone who has a general knowledge of Teams, or has completed Microsoft Teams Level 100 training, and is looking to increase their skill set.

To view the recording:

  * Go to the WTS [Microsoft Teams](https://wts.uwo.ca/microsoft_teams/index.html) website
  * Select _Microsoft Teams Level 200 Training Session - August 2021_



You may review the Microsoft Teams Level 100 training recording above.

### Zoom

**Resources:**

[Zoom at Western](https://wts.uwo.ca/zoom/index.html)  
[Zoom Privacy Considerations](https://wts.uwo.ca/zoom/privacy_considerations/index.html)  
[Zoom Interactive Tools Quick Guide for Facilitators](../../form_doc/learning/zoom_quick_guide.pdf)  
[Summary of Interactive Tools for Zoom Meeting](../../form_doc/learning/zoom_tool_summary.pdf)

**Workshop:**

**Audience:** Western Staff & Faculty  
**Workshop Leader:** Blair Trewartha, Learning Specialist, Human Resources & Financial Services  
  
In this virtual workshop participants will learn how to make meetings interactive and engaging using a variety of tools available in Zoom.   
The best use for each tool, how to set it up, and keys for success will be highlighted.  
  
Topics include:   
\- Zoom Interactive Tools - Chat, Raise Hand, Polls, Screen Share, Breakout Rooms  
\- Keys to Success - Preparation, Technology, Backup Plans

**Length:** 90 minutes

**Scheduled for:** TBD

**Course Number:** MLDZ
