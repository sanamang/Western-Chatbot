# Co-op Program

The Science Co-op Program at Western University provides undergraduate Science students with the opportunity to gain valuable, paid, full-time work experience. Through this program, eligible students can complete up to 20 months of co-op work, which includes an optional 4-month summer term after their second year and a 16-month window for co-op opportunities between their third and fourth years. Work terms are structured in 4-month increments (4, 8, 12, or 16 months) based on employer needs, and students must complete a minimum of 12 months of cumulative work experience to fulfill the program requirements. After completing their final co-op term, students return to campus to finish their four-year Science degree.
