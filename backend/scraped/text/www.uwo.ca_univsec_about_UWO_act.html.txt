# The University of Western Ontario Act

### Unofficial Consolidation

The University of Western Ontario Act, 1982 was amended in 1988 and 2016. In addition, under the provisions of Section 24.(2) of the Act, changes to the composition of Senate were introduced, effective July 1, 1997. An unofficial consolidation of the 1982 Act, incorporating all amendedments and changes, as well as changes to Faculty names and other administrative details, is provided below.

**[Unofficial Consolidation of The University of Western Ontario Act](../pdf/about/university_act/unofficial_consolidation.pdf)**

A list of amendments and changes to the act is provided [here](../pdf/about/university_act/Amendments.pdf).

### 1996 Mandated Review

A Mandated Review of the Act was held in 1996. The final report of that review can be accessed [here](final_report/index.html).
