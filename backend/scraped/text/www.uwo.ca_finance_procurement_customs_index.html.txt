# Customs & Logistics

Our team manages the University’s customs and logistics needs. We serve as a resource for information on: 

  * Selection of carriers and couriers,
  * Import and export process, restrictions, permits and licenses,
  * Determining landed costs of goods, where required for funding and grant applications.
