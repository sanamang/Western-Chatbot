# Who We Are

In This Section

## World-Class Academics

At Western, students learn from leading researchers and scholars across 11 faculties and schools, gaining insights and engaging with cutting-edge ideas. Professors are leaders in their subject matter and model a commitment to excellence that inspires and motivates students in their own academic and professional endeavours.

  
[Learn More](../../academics/index.html)

  


* * *

## Student Experience

From admission to graduation, we’re here to help you thrive within and beyond the classroom – academically, mentally, physically and socially – and into the future. Find community. Discover supports and opportunities. Make a difference. Your student experience at Western can transform your life and the world around you.

  
[Learn More](https://studentexperience.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Research Impact

Western is among the top research-intensive universities in Canada, known for its world-class facilities and expertise across disciplines. From fundamental research to applied discovery, our researchers and scholars are advancing knowledge and making a positive impact in Canada and worldwide.

  
[Learn More](https://www.uwo.ca/research/)

  


* * *

## International Opportunities

Internationalization is an integral part of Western's academic mission. We strive to create a welcoming and globally engaged environment, supporting international students, facilitating study abroad experiences, fostering international partnerships and promoting cross-cultural understanding among the university community.

  
[Learn More](https://international.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Alumni Community

Western graduates are highly sought after both nationally and internationally. Our alumni are part of a global network of accomplished professionals who are leaders in their fields and communities. Events, services and lifelong learning programs enable alumni to stay connected, develop new skills and celebrate enduring bonds formed during their time at Western.

  
[Learn More](https://alumni.westernu.ca/)

  


* * *

## Vibrant Campus

Western’s 1,300+ acre campus is one of the most beautiful in Canada, offering a mixture of modern and historic architecture and over 1,200 species of animals and plants. Twelve on-campus residences provide a safe, comfortable and welcoming living community for students and scholars. 

  
[Learn More](../visit/index.html)

  


* * *

## Welcoming City

London is a vibrant, thriving city located in Southwestern Ontario. Known for its rich cultural heritage and strong sense of community, London offers a blend of urban amenities, natural spaces and attractions, and a lively arts and entertainment scene – enjoyed by residents and visitors alike.

  
[Learn More](../visit/london.html)
