# Research Support Team

The FHS Research Team, situated in the office of the Associate Dean (Research), provides strategic direction, administrative support, and expertise across the research lifecycle.

The team is located on the fourth floor of the Arthur and Sonia Labatt Health Sciences Building, in suites 411 and 417. Reach the team at [fhsresearch@uwo.ca](mailto:fhsresearch@uwo.ca)
