# News & Announcements

## Call for Nominations: Western Awards for Excellence in Teaching 

The Senate Committee on University Teaching Awards is now accepting nominations for the Western Awards for Excellence in Teaching. Nominations must be submitted to [teaching.awards@uwo.ca](mailto:teaching.awards@uwo.ca) by January 15, 2026. 

In addition, nominations for the Vice-Provost's Awards for Excellence in Teaching may be submitted to [ahitch2@uwo.ca](mailto:ahitch2@uwo.ca) by January 15, 2026.

For further information, please review the [Call for Nominations](pdf/senate/callnoms.pdf). 

* * *

## Board of Governors By-Election - Undergraduate Student Constituency

Polls have now closed to elect one undergraduate student representative to the Board of Governors for a term from October 23, 2025 to June 30, 2026. The results are available on the [Board of Govenors elections webpage](https://www.uwo.ca/univsec/board/elections.html). 

* * *

## **Call for Nominations: University Discipline Appeal Committee**

The Board of Governors' Senior Policy and Operations Committee is accepting nominations from members of the University community for membership on the University Discipline Appeal Committee. Visit [Call for Nominations](pdf/board/new-noms.pdf) for details on the current vacancies and the process for submitting a nomination.

* * *

## **Call for Nominations for Membership on Senate Committees**

The Senate Nominating Committee is accepting nominations from members of the University community for membership on Senate committees. Visit [Call for Nominations for Senate Committees](https://www.uwo.ca/univsec/senate/committees/call_for_nominations.html) for details on current vacancies and the process for submitting a nomination.
