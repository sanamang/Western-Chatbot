# Log in to Your Pension Plan Account with Sun Life

**Active** Western employees may use the link below, and their Western user ID and password to access their Western Pension Plan account at Sun Life Financial. Access is now secured with Duo Security. In the event Duo Security is experiencing an outage, please see the alternative sign in method below. For more information or troubleshooting on DUO MFA, please visit the [WTS MFA webpage](https://mfa.uwo.ca/).

## [**Use your Western credentials to login at Sun Life**](https://sso-dcc11a82.sso.duosecurity.com/saml2/sp/DIFVNNPTF2O6LCFX32F9/sso)

**All members** , including those who are retired and those who left Western before retirement may login directly at [**mysunlife.ca/western**](http://www.sunlife.ca/western). 

First time accessing your Sun Life account? Follow the Sun Life link and select Register. [Read more about registration](sun_life.html#register).
