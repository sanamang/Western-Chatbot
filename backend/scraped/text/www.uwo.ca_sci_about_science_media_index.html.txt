# Media

The Faculty of Science at Western is comprised of a community of more than 6,000 faculty, staff, and students. These are focused, driven, and multidimensional personalities, each with a story to tell.

Whether you are interested in the latest research developments in environment and sustainability, information science, or material science; need expert insight or commentary on current events and issues facing society; or want to develop a human interest piece, Science Communications is happy to work with you to bring the best of our community to your audience.
