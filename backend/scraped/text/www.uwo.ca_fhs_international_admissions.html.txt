# Admissions

## **Undergraduate Programs**

A competitive high school average, specified requirements for your program of choice, and [English Language Proficiency](https://welcome.uwo.ca/next-steps/requirements/english-language-proficiency.html) (if English is not your first language). [See undergraduate entrance requirements for your country](https://welcome.uwo.ca/next-steps/requirements/international-high-school/index.html).

## **Graduate Programs**

**Master's** : Applicants must possess a four-year degree from a university, college or institute. The School of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies requires at least a 70% average across courses taken in the last two full-time years of the undergraduate degree. Equivalent qualifications may be considered based on the standards of the discipline or profession.

**Doctoral** : Applicants must possess a Master’s degree or equivalent from a university, college or institute, and provide evidence of research potential. The School of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies requires at least a 70% average in the Master’s degree, as determined by the School of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies.
