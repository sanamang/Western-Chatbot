# Advanced Health Care Practice (Master's)

**The first program of its kind in Canada** , the Advanced Health Care Practice program is a one-year course-based master's program offering experienced health-care professionals an opportunity to obtain graduate-level education in a unique field of study. This master's-level program uses a variety of approaches to teaching and learning and utilizes the critical appraisal of research evidence.

### The AHCP program offers **clinical** and **applied** fields of study:

## Clinical Fields

  * [Clinical Epidemiology and Research Management](cerm/index.html)
  * [Sport and Exercise Medicine](sport_ex/index.html)
  * [Upper Extremity Rehabilitation](upper_rehab/index.html)
  * [Comprehensive Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy](cmp/index.html)
  * [Wound Healing](wound/index.html)
  * [Interdisciplinary Pain Management](pain/index.html)



## Applied Fields

  * [Health Equity and Sustainability](hes/index.html)
  * [Applied Health Sciences](../ahs/index.html)[](https://welcome.uwo.ca/int-high-school-students.html)



* * *

### [Clinical Epidemiology and Research Management](cerm/index.html)

Designed to help graduates develop the skills they need to build and lead research teams and advance evidence-based thinking in their field.

[Learn More](cerm/index.html)

* * *

### [Sport and Exercise Medicine](sport_ex/index.html)

Designed to prepare physiotherapists and physicians in the pillars of advanced practice and provide advanced clinical care in sport injury and exercise medicine environments.

[Learn More](sport_ex/index.html)

* * *

### [Upper Extremity Rehabilitation](upper_rehab/index.html)

Positions graduates to effectively manage the complexity of clinical presentatations and enable best practice in upper extremity rehabilitation.[](https://westernuniversity.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJMvcuqqqDosHN1wlPPLmy9d2wpwFrDj4awx)

[Learn More](upper_rehab/index.html)

* * *

### [Comprehensive Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy](cmp/index.html)

This program prepares physiotherapists to be advanced practice clinicians in the assessment and treatment of neuromuscular dysfunction and manage complex musculoskeletal clinical conditions.

[Learn More](cmp/index.html)

* * *

### [Wound Healing](wound/index.html)

[](/programs/ahcp/wound "Wound Healing")

Focuses on the development of specialized clinical skills and research methods needed to assess and treat people with acute and chronic wounds.

[Learn More](wound/index.html)

* * *

### [Interdisciplinary Pain Management](pain/index.html)

[](pain/index.html "Interdisciplinary Pain Management")

Designed to help healthcare professionals develop advanced skills in managing complex pain by using their own real-world experiences as a platform for learning.

[Learn More](pain/index.html)

* * *

### [Health Equity and Sustainability](hes/index.html)

Prepares graduates to address environmental, socio-economic, and political factors that shape health across the life course. 

[Learn More](hes/index.html)

* * *

### [Applied Health Sciences](../ahs/masters.html)

[](../ahs/masters.html "Applied Health Sciences")

This fully-online graduate program encourages students to leverage their own experience and emphasizes authentic, case-based learning. The program’s foundations are health promotion and education, evidence-informed health interventions and effective supports for individuals throughout the lifespan.

[Learn More](../ahs/masters.html)
