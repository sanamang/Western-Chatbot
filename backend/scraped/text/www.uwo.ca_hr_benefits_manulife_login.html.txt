# Manulife Benefits Login

Please use the link below to log into your personal Manulife account. To login as a registered member, you will need your Manulife Plan contract number (87220), your Member certificate number (this is the employee number found on your Western ONECard) and your password.

## [Go to the Manulife login page](https://id.manulife.ca/signin)

**Not yet registered?** To register for online access go to the [Manulife website](https://id.manulife.ca/signin) to setup your Manulife ID or connect your Western coverage to your established Manulife ID.

Further resources regarding setup of your Manulife ID and connecting to your Western coverage can be found at:

**[Group Benefits Manulife ID support webpage](https://www.manulife.ca/personal/support/manulife-id/group-benefits.html?scid=eml-ext:ManulifeID:::::::7647949&rid=400914504)**

Once registered, you may login to view the status of your most recent claims, submit online claims, review your claim history, download a digital benefits card, download claim forms, check your HCSA and WSA balances (if applicable), lookup drug eligibility, set up direct deposit and access the Manulife Wellness Centre.

**Mobile App** : Once you've registered you can also install the Manulife mobile app for your smartphone or tablet so you may submit and review claims on the go. The mobile app can be downloaded from the Apple App Store, Google Play or the Windows Store.

## Related Benefit Information

Looking for more information about your Benefits? The navigation links on the left will take you to our most accessed Benefit pages. In addition, the following may be helpful:

  * **Get help with login, claims, or questions** \- If you need assistance with your login, your claims, or if you have a question about your Health Care or Dental Benefits, please **call 1-866-896-8515, or use the live chat function on the Manulife website** and a Manulife representative will be pleased to assist you.
  * **[Professional Expense Reimbursement (PER) Account](https://www.uwo.ca/finance/accounting/professional_expense_reimbursement.html)** – Check your balance, make claims, and access general information.
