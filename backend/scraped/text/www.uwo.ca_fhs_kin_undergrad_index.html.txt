### Undergraduate Program Information

For information about undergraduate programs in Kinesiology, contact the Undergraduate Programs Office:

Email: [kinug@uwo.ca](mailto:kinug@uwo.ca)

# Undergraduate Programs

From human movement and rehabilitation to fitness and exercise, sport management to the cultural impact of sport, Western Kinesiology has it covered with a balanced approach designed to help students become leaders in their field.

## Admissions

[](admissions.html "admissions")   
  
The first stop for students interested in joining Western's School of Kinesiology. Get information about admission requirements, prerequisite courses, application procedures/deadlines and more. 

[ Learn More ](admissions.html "admissions")

## Your First Year

[](first_year.html "your first year")  
  
Undergraduate students in the School of Kinesiology take a common set of first-year courses that help them identify areas of interest and in selecting a program/module prior to entering second year. 

[ Learn More ](first_year.html "your first year")

## Programs

  
  
Kinesiology at Western is not a one-size-fits-all program. Through a series of courses and modules or combined degree programs, students can tailor their program of study to meet their individual learning goals. 

[ Learn More ](programs/index.html "programs")

## Academic Advising

  
  
Academic advisors in the School of Kinesiology are available to help students get the most out of their academic careers and provide information and advice regarding a variety of academic issues. 

[ Learn More ](academic/index.html "academic counselling")
