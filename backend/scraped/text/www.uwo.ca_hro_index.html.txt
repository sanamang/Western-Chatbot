Human Rights Office

## Welcome

The Human Rights Office supports Western’s respectful and inclusive working and learning environment by providing advice and guidance on Western’s Non-Discrimination, Harassment and Sexual Misconduct Policy. We are dedicated to making Western a safe, respectful and supportive environment for all members of the university community.

## How We Can Help You

### Report a Concern

#### 

The Western Human Rights Office (HRO) is here to help. We provide advice and support to all members of Western's campus community: students, staff, faculty, and leaders.

[Read More](https://uwo.ca/hro/discrimination/help/index.html)

### Policies & Procedures

#### 

Western has a number of policies related to equity and human rights issues. The University also has agreements with several unions and employee groups to include related articles in their collective or employment agreements.

[Read More](https://uwo.ca/hro/about/policies.html)

### Learning Resources

#### 

Each member of our campus community is encouraged to help make Western an equitable, safe and inclusive environment. Here are some resources to help.
