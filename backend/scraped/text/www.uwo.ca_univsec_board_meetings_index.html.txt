# Meetings of the Board of Governors

Board meetings are held on **Thursdays at 10:00 a.m.** in the Western Interdisciplinary Research Building (WIRB), Room 3000, unless otherwise noted. Members of the public who wish to attend the Open Session virtually are invited to contact the University Secretary at [secretariat@uwo.ca](mailto:secretariat-covid19@uwo.ca).

Agendas are normally posted on the [**Agendas and Minutes**](agendas_minutes.html) webpage a week in advance of the meeting.

**2025**

  * February 6
  * May 1
  * June 26
  * September 25
  * November 20

  


**2026**

  * February 5
  * April 30
  * June 18
