### News

**Bernd Steinbock** presented a paper entitled “Athenian Responses to the Disaster in Sicily as Indicators of a Social Trauma” at the International Workshop [_Emotions of Crisis_](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/archaeology-classics-and-egyptology/events/emotions-of-crisis-workshop/) at the University of Liverpool. As well, he attended the 9th Annual Meeting of the [ _Memory Studies Association_](https://msaprague2025.dryfta.com/index.php) in Prague with a presentation on “Thucydides, LaCapra, and Empathic Unsettlement in Recounting Traumatic Events.”

* * *

**Beth Greene** has received a Western Research Excellence Award (Outstanding Scholar) for 2025. Congratulations on this incredible achievement! Read the Western News article [here](https://news.westernu.ca/2025/07/western-research-excellence-awards-2025/). 

* * *

Congratulations to**Angela Weiler** (HSP), the first Western student to receive the Archaeological Institute of America's Jane C. Waldbaum Field School Scholarship. The full announcement can be found [here](https://www.archaeological.org/2025-aia-field-school-scholarship-winners-announcement/). 

* * *

**Beth Greene** is featured in a news story about the discovery of exceptionally large ancient Roman shoes along Hadrian's Wall near Vindolanda. This story has appeared across multiple news outlets globally, including [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/giant-shoes-found-near-hadrians-wall-spark-mystery-around-the-soldiers-of-ancient-rome/) and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwyk3kjp4w3o). 

* * *

**Aara Suksi** wins the [Edward G. Pleva Award for Excellence in Teaching](https://news.westernu.ca/2025/04/western-awards-excellence-teaching/). Congratulations, Dr. Suksi!

* * *

**Jenna Colclough** (MA 2019) publishes  _[Our Little Agonies](https://www.jencolclough.com/the-poetry-collection)_ , her debut poetry collection.
