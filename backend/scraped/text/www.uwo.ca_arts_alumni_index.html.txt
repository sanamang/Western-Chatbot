# Alumni

Arts & Humanities alumni are a part of Western's family of over 372,481 alumni in over 160 countries around the world. We love hearing what you are up to since your days on campus. Join us for upcoming events, drop us a line through [email](mailto:%20jo.jennings@uwo.ca) and social media, and stay connected with us! Visit [Western's Alumni website](https://alumni.westernu.ca/) for more info on how you can get involved, purple perks, career workshops, and more!
