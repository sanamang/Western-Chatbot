# Institutional Projects - Post Award

This section describes the post award process at Western for the Canadian Foundation of Innovation (CFI) including the Infrastructure Operating Fund (CFI-IOF) and for the Ontario Research Excellence (ORF-RE) awards. 

These projects are major institutional awards that may include industry partners and other institutions. Due to the spending and reporting restrictions these projects are monitored very closely by Research Finance. Continual communication and co-ordination between researchers, project team members, partner institutions, and related administrative staff is required.

For application and award finalization please contact [Research Development and Services](https://www.uwo.ca/research/about/adr_facilitator_contacts.html).
