# Service Vehicle Parking

Western Service Vehicles can park in signed Service Vehicle spaces, as well as any regular permit or visitor space.

**Service vehicles may not be park in fire routes or reserved spaces.**

Click on a lot/area name to find the location of Service Vehicle parking spaces.

### Alumni/Thompson (Lot M) - Visitor Lot

Service Vehicles spaces are located on the side of the lot closest to Huron College.

### Bayfield and Ausable Hall Residences Area

Service Vehicles spaces are located on the side of the lot closest to Huron College.

### B & G Greenhouses 

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Essex Hall 

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Ivey Lot

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Lambton Lot (L)

Service vehicle spaces are located on the South side of the Lambton Residence.

### Medical Science Lot (C)

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the building near the accessible spaces.

### Ontario Hall Lot (N)

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Support Services Lot (K)

Service Vehicles spaces are located on the side of the lot closest to Huron College.

### University Drive

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Upper Heating

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.

### Visual Arts

Service vehicle spaces are located along the south side of the lot facing the building.
