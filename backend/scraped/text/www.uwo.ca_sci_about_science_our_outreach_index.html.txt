# Our Outreach

Students and faculty take an active part in sharing their passion for science beyond campus. Our extensive Outreach program collaborates with community organisations and schools to engage with London families, friends and future students interested in science at community venues and on campus throughout the year. From tours, public presentations and night-sky observations at the Cronyn Observatory, to workshops, laboratory demonstrations and competitions, Western Science opens its doors regularly to bring the community to Science and science to our community.

We invite you to explore the organisations with whom we have had the pleasure of collaborating and some featured activities that we have supported!
