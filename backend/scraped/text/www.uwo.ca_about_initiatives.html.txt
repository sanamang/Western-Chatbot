# Major Initiatives

In This Section

## Equity, Diversity & Inclusion

Western's Office of Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) celebrates and recognizes diverse communities on campus, hosting engaging events, designing resources and providing learning opportunities for all students, staff, faculty and alumni. The Office of EDI fosters a welcoming and supportive environment for all members of the university community, ensuring Western is a more inclusive and welcoming campus.

  
[Learn More](https://www.edi.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Indigenous Initiatives

Western’s Office of Indigenous Initiatives (OII) leads system-wide changes to advance Truth and Reconciliation and achieve equitable outcomes for Indigenous people. OII champions Indigenous strategic directions, fosters collaborative and community-engaged partnerships and supports campus partners in implementing Western’s Indigenous Strategic Plan.

  
[Learn More](https://indigenous.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Sustainability

Western is committed to advancing all aspects of sustainability, including economic, social and environmental. We have set an ambitious target to be net zero by 2050 and continue to make investments that move us closer to that goal.

  
[Learn More](https://sustainability.uwo.ca/index.html)

  


* * *

## Wellness & Well-being

Western offers information to help direct students, faculty, staff, parents and families to health and wellness services and resources available at Western.

  
[Learn More](https://www.uwo.ca/health//index.html)

  


* * *

## Campus Safety

Community safety is of utmost importance to Western. Whether it's Special Constable Service, Fire Safety, the Student Emergency Response Team, or the Western Foot Patrol, Western has services intended to help keep campus a safe place to learn, work, and play.

  
[Learn More](../campus_life/campus-safety.html)

  


* * *

## Accessibility

Western has a variety of services, groups and committees on campus to ensure barrier-free accessibility, from physical to online presence for persons studying, working or visiting campus.

  
[Learn More](https://accessibility.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Entrepreneurship

Western’s Morrissette Institute for Entrepreneurship Powered by Ivey provides the tools, knowledge, and support to turn innovative ideas into successful ventures. Open to all members of our Western community, the institute offers courses, certificates, experiential programs, access to maker spaces, mentorship, funding opportunities, awards, and scholarships.

  
[Learn More](https://entrepreneurship.uwo.ca/)

  


* * *

## Accountability

Western is committed to openness and transparency in all our operations. We are supported by and accountable to the people of Ontario and of Canada.

  
[Learn More](https://www.uwo.ca/ipb/publicaccountability/)
