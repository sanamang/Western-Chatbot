# Accessibility

Western is committed to increasing accessibility for persons with disabilities who come here to learn, work, or visit. A number of policies, resources, and services are available to support accessibility at Western. Please visit [Western's Accessibility website](https://accessibility.uwo.ca) for more information.

Supporting this commitment, Western **Human Resources** strives to make our services accessible.

### Access to Human Resource Offices - Room 4159 Support Services Building

We are located in Western’s Support Services Building, in the north-west area of campus. Accessible parking is available on the south side of the building. Our main reception area is on the same level as the Western Road building entrance. Accessible washrooms are available; service animals and support persons are welcome. There are two public access computers within our reception area.

### Contacting Us, Directions to Campus, Maps

For Human Resource contact information or directions to campus go to: [Contact Us](../contact.html)

For a campus accessibility map go to the [the Western Accessibility maps site.](https://www.accessibility.uwo.ca/resources/maps/index.html "Accessibility maps")

### Documents and Forms

Please [contact us](../contact.html) if you are unable to access the documents and/or forms found on this website or if you require the information in an alternate format.

### Feedback

We welcome feedback about our Human Resource services. Please use [Contact Us](../contact.html) to ask a question, offer feedback, or to let us know if any other arrangements can make our services accessible to you.  
  
Feedback about accessibility related matters may also be provided through [Western’s Accessibility Feedback Process](https://accessibility.uwo.ca/resources/feedback.html).

### Service Disruptions

In the event of a closure or other disruption of Western Human Resource Services, we will provide notice using:

  * [Human Resources website](../index.html)
  * Door signs
  * And, when appropriate, a notice on Western's general [Service Disruption website](http://www.accessibility.uwo.ca/resources/disruptions/index.html "Service Disruptions")



We will indicate the reason for the disruption, the anticipated duration, and alternatives for service, if available.

### Web Accessibility

Web accessibility is the inclusive practicе of making websites usable by people of all abilities and disabilities. Western Communications and Public Affairs offers [information to assist you in achieving accessibility](https://www.communications.uwo.ca/digital-resources/web-accessibility-standards-aoda.html).
