# Alumni Award of Achievement

** **

### Previous Awardees

2022: [Amy Gottschling](../news/2022/gottschling-alumni-award.html)

2023: [Boluwaji Ogunyemi](../news/2023/Dr-Boluwaji-Ogunyemi-Alumni-Award-Winner.html)

2024: [Amanda Holden](../news/2024/Amanda Holden Alumni Award Winner.html)

2025: [Nigel Branker](../news/2025/Nigel-Branker-Alumni-Award-Winner.html)

**Celebrating our alumni’s outstanding professional contributions.**

All over the world, Western Science Alumni are doing incredible things to propel their coworkers, organizations, and industries forward.

We want to recognize them.

The Western Science Alumni Award of Achievement, created in 2022, acknowledges and celebrates our alumni who have made outstanding contributions to their professions.

### Who can be nominated?

Alumni of the Faculty of Science at Western University who:

  * Work in a science or non-science related profession
  * Have made a significant contribution to their profession
  * Demonstrate leadership in their professional role and act as role models to those around them
  * Inspire others to achieve excellence



Do you know someone like this? Nominate them today!

**Nominations for the 2025 Western Science Alumni Award of Achievement are closed.  
**

The deadline to submit nominations was Monday, March 31, 2025. The winner will be announced in the summer of 2025 and will be invited to attend an awards banquet to be recognized and in celebration with fellow alumni.

  


* * *

#### Questions?

Please contact [Karmen Ferraro.](mailto:karmen.ferraro@uwo.ca)

_Note: Applications and letters of support are confidential and will be viewed only by members of the Selection Committee. The Selection Committee will be comprised of members of the Western Science community._

_We welcome self-nominations and nominations of others, however, posthumous nominations will not be accepted. It is recommended that the nomination remain confidential and not shared with the nominee_.
