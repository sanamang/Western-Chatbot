# Student Parking Permits

If you are a student on campus who is also employed by Western (TA, RA, etc.), your student status overrides any Employee status. Students in this position are eligible for a student permit, not an Employee permit.

Student permits can be purchased online once you've set-up a Parking Account. Click on the 'Need Help?' section on the bottom left of this page for help with creating your Parking Account.

**Student permits are limited and are sold based on available space. We encourage students living in residence or close to campus to forego bringing a vehicle to campus.**

_Students are reminded that purchasing Parking Permits through any third-party source (Kijiji, TikTok, Facebook Marketplace etc.) is contrary to Parking Regulations and students could be held responsible for any tickets associated with the permit they are using. Additional sanctions against their Western Parking Account could also apply._

Western Parking Permits should be purchased online here when available:

**The Fall 2025 (September 1st to December 31st) and Annual 2025/26 (September 1st 2025 to August 31st 2026) Student Parking Permits are sold out as of July 4 2025.**

The next available Student Parking Permits which will be available for sale will be for the Winter Term (Jan 2026 – Apr 2026) and are scheduled to be available for sale on November 15 2025.

We will be running a waitlist for the Annual and Fall Term Student Parking Permits and movement on the waitlist will directly depend on how many of the Annual and Fall Term Student Parking Permits which were sold are returned to the parking office. 

If you would like to be added to the waitlist, please select the [following link](https://uwo.eu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3aBbjolcUgkVyXY).

**Hang-tags can also be picked up early at the Parking Office during normal operating hours.**

### Student Parking Zones

Parking permits are not sold by lot; they are sold by zone. Students occupy the [green zone](../../find/map.html "See Parking Map"), which is comprised of:

  * Althouse West (**Tip:** fills to capacity by about 10:00 am) 
  * Bayfield Student Lot
  * Clare Hall Lot
  * Ontario Hall Student Lot
  * Medway (**Tip:** does not usually fill to capacity)
  * Huron Flats (**Tip:** fills to capacity in September because of football)
  * Springett Student Lot (**Tip:** fills to capacity by about 8:30 am) 
  * West Campus Lot
  * South Valley Lot - student permits can park in the South Valley Lot between the hours of 9:30 am and 7 am the next day. GNR/Student permits must not be in the lot between the hours of 7 am and 9:30 am (M-F) in the morning, to prioritize employee parking. 
  * Westminster Student Lot  


  


Periodically student permit holders are given access to additional parking lots on a temporary basis due to special events and construction on campus. Permit holders are asked to obey posted signage as lot access schedules are subject to change.

### Student Permit Refund Policy

### Administration Fee on Permit Refunds 

There are no administrative fees for refunding student permits.

### Returning a Term or Annual Permit Early 

Students who purchase a term or annual permit and return the permit early will be charged the monthly rate for the period they held the permit for, and will no longer be eligible for the cost savings associated with the purchase of a term/annual permit.

### Cut Off Dates for Refund of Permits 

Students returning permits after the 1st of the month will be charged for that current month.

The last day for the return of a _fall term_ permit for partial refund is December 1st. 

The last day for the return of an _annual permit_ for partial refund is April 1st.

The last day for the return of a _winter term_ permit for partial refund is April 1st.

### Hang Tag Deposit 

The $30 hang tag deposit fee is always returned to customers with no outstanding balances on their parking account. Photo identification is required to obtain a refund.
