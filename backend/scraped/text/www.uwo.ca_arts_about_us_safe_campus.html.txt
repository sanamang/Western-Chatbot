# Safe Campus Community

### University College Building Emergency Procedures

Our building emergency team will assist with evacuating the building during emergencies. 

### **When alarm sounds**

  * Grab your coat, keys, purse or wallet -- you may not be allowed back in the building
  * Close door of office/room
  * Evacuate the building immediately by the stairs (do not use the elevators)
  * If unable to take the stairs down, wait in the stairwell. A building emergency team member will notify the fire department. If you have a cell phone on you, call 911 to let them know your location
  * Once outside the building, be sure to stand at least 10 metres from the building exit
  * Wait until the alarm stops and you get the ok from a building emergency team member to go back in the building
