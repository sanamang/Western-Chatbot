### Quick Service Overview

### Academic Support

What do you if you miss course work? Where can you find information regarding your program and Western’s policies?

Your first stop for any academic inquiries or other questions should be the [ **Science/BMSc Academic Advising Office** ](https://www.uwo.ca/sci/counselling/) in the North Campus Building, Room 280.

We can help you:

  * If you miss a class

  * Want to book an appointment

  * Need information on graduating on time




Be sure to check out our [ **office hours** ](https://www.uwo.ca/sci/counselling/advising_services/counselling_hours.html) and frequently asked questions. Follow the team on Twitter **[@westernuSCIAC](https://twitter.com/westernusciac "@WesternUSciac") **for important deadlines, up to date information, and much more!

### Healthy Body and Mind

Western is committed to helping our students maintain a healthy mind and body. **[Student Health](http://www.uwo.ca/health/ "Student Health") ** is available to all Western Science students should you be feeling ill, have missed course work and require medical documentation, or wish to be referred to a psychologist or mental health specialist.

The Science Academic Advising team invites you to take part in Wellness Wednesday! Take a mini-break and join us each week for snacks and fun activities. Watch for signs, tweets, and emails about upcoming Wellness Wednesday events. All the events are free!

### Student Professional and Career Development

From the beginning of first-year, we advise that you start looking beyond your undergraduate degree and plan the professional path you hope to follow after graduation. **[Western Science Career Services](../careers_and_internships/index.html "Western Career Services") **points you to the resources and opportunities that you need to succeed, from online workshops which develop core corporate competencies, to finding an internship opportunity in order to 'try on' a career. 

The Science Internship Program, offered by Western Science Career Services, provides senior undergraduate students support to develop the skills and tools you need to secure an 8-16-month placement in industry, government, or the not-for-profit sector. The internship experience demands the application of your science background while providing ample opportunity to develop the professional competencies and networks you require to work effectively individually or as a member of a team in the public or private sector or even in academia.

Explore your interests early. Determine your strengths and develop confidence in your ability to deliver in a professional setting. We are here to help you reach your potential.
