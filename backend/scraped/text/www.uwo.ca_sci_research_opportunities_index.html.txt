### **Undergraduate Student Positions**

#### NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Awards (USRA)

If you would like to gain research work experience that complements your studies in an academic setting, USRAs can provide you with financial support through your host institution. 

More Information:

[Research Western USRA Information](https://www.uwo.ca/research/funding/students/nserc_usra_overview.html)

[NSERC USRA Page](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/UG-PC/USRA-BRPC_eng.asp)

Please see your Department pages for more information about undergraduate research opportunities:

  * [Biology](https://uwo.ca/biology/undergraduate/current-students/awards-and-research-opportunities.html)
  * [Chemistry](https://uwo.ca/chem/undergraduate/current_students/scholarships_and_awards.html)
  * [Computer Science](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/undergraduate/index.html)



  * [Earth Sciences](https://www.uwo.ca/earth/undergraduate/scholarships_and_awards/index.html)
  * [Mathematics](https://uwo.ca/math/undergraduate/current_students/summer_research.html)
  * [Physics and Astronomy](https://physics.uwo.ca/undergraduate/current_students/undergraduate_research_topics/index.html)
  * [Statistical and Actuarial Sciences](https://www.uwo.ca/stats/undergraduate/USRI+USRA.html)



For Post-Doctoral Scholars, please see **[Research Western](https://www.uwo.ca/research/funding/students/postdoctoral_fellowship_program.html)**. Graduate Students, prospective or current, are encouraged to explore our Department pages and to reach out to individual professors or research groups.
