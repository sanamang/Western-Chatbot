# Visitor Parking

There is no complimentary parking on campus during the week Monday to Friday, 7 am to 5 pm. Evening and weekend complimentary parking locations can be found under [Summer, Evenings and Weekends](../summer_evening_weekend.html). Visitors and invited guests should be directed to one of our available visitor attended lots, Honk Mobile areas, or Pay & Display metered areas. All parking on campus is restricted to lots/spaces that have been designated for various permits, etc. The University campus is private property; valid permits or paid meter receipts must be visible on your dashboard.

#### [**Attended Lot Visitor Parking**](attended.html)

Credit card payment only.

  * [See Rates & Locations](attended.html)



#### [**Permit/Visitor Lots**](permit_visitor_parking_lots.html)

Visitors are required to pay by credit card based on time in a lot.

  * [See Rates & Locations](permit_visitor_parking_lots.html)



#### [**Honk Mobile/Pay & Display**](metered.html)

No daily maximum cost. Pay & Display Units accept Visa, MasterCard, Amex and Visa Debit as well as coin ($2, $1 and quarters).

  * [See Rates & Locations](metered.html)



  


### Student Residence - Guest Parking 

Overnight guests of students in residence should park in a paid Visitor Lot, Pay and Display or Honk Mobile area, or may be able to take advantage of free overnight parking on the weekends in the [Huron Flats](../map.html) parking lot.
