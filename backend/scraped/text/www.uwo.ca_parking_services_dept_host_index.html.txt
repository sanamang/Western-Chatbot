# Department Hosted Parking

### 

### [Department-Hosted Visitor Parking](Guest Parking Portal.html)

Print or email guest parking passes for your visitors. Valid for pay and display areas, attended visitor lots as well as gated visitor lots.   
  
  


### 

###  [](special_events.html)

### [Special Event Parking](special_events.html)

When your department is expecting 25 or more guest parkers on one day, you are required to connect with our Special Event Coordinator to ensure campus has parking capacity when and where you need it.
