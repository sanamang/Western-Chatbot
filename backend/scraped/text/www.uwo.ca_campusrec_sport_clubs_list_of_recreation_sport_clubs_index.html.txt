Some Clubs may offer additional features such as instructional classes for skill development and competitive opportunities for those who chose to participate. **LEGEND:** ***** \- _New club as of the current year._ **R** \- _This Club is entirely**recreational**. May or may not have internal competitions within the Club._ **I** \-  _This Club is recreational in nature with an**instructional** component, meaning an instructor leads classes for skill development._ **C** \- _This Club is recreational in nature but also offers**competitive** opportunities for Club members to participate in open tournaments or against other universities. _ __

### Recreational Sport Clubs Schedule (2025-2026)

**SPORT CLUBS WEEK**

September 15-19 in the WSRC Atrium

  * **Sunday, September 21 st** \- Start of Sport Clubs Season
  * **Monday, October 13 th** \- Thanksgiving Monday (_no sessions_)
  * **Sunday, November 2 nd to Saturday, November 8th (_inclusive_) **\- Fall Reading Week Break

  * **Saturday, December 6 th** \- Last day of sessions for the Fall Term




WINTER HOLIDAY BREAK

  * **Sunday, January 18 th **\- Beginning of Winter Term; Sport Clubs sessions resume
  * **Sunday, February 15 th to Saturday, February 21st (_inclusive_)** \- Winter Reading Week Break

  * **Friday, April 3 rd **\- Good Friday (_no sessions_)
  * **Saturday, April 4 th** \- End of Sport Clubs Season
