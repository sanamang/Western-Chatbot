# DesignEd: Course Design Support

**DesignEd is** a curriculum and educational media development team that collaborates with instructors to enhance teaching and learning across FHS. We are focused on the design, development, and delivery of learning experiences that integrate curricular innovation, student-centred pedagogies, and media-rich learning assets.

### [OWL Sakai/Brightspace + Educational Technology Support](brightspace_support/index.html)

Need help migrating and preparing your course on OWL? Want to resize or sharpen an image for your course? Have questions about structuring your course or using groups, quizzes, forums, assignments, lesson pages, etc? [Book an online OWL or ed tech support session](https://outlook.office365.com/owa/calendar/DesignEdTeam1@uwoca.onmicrosoft.com/bookings/s/SEcy9KlTnEOnD9R1e8D7bw2) or [submit a support request ticket via Jira](https://westernu.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/35/group/141/create/641).

### [Curriculum and Course Development Support](project_requests.html)

Need video or audio production support? Want help designing a new course or improving an existing one? Thinking about enhancing your course's accessibility? Want to brainstorm about an assessment? [Book an appointment to chat about your curriculum needs](https://outlook.office365.com/owa/calendar/DesignEdTeam1@uwoca.onmicrosoft.com/bookings/s/o9yAVDU7kUCiieQ_XuWxBw2) or [fill out a project request form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=TaaTrQ2tzU6y_eU84Vllvo_CMlvQjoVKoqZXCgcUbTJUNElRMFJOVjBJR1Q0NDFNMUszTkhMT0pGUC4u) and we'll get back to you.
