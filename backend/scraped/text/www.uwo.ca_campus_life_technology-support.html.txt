# Technology Support

In This Section

[Western Technology Services (WTS)](https://wts.uwo.ca/) is the go-to destination for all your technology needs at Western University! WTS is dedicated to supporting students, faculty, and staff through information technology services. We keep you connected through our networks, safeguard your online presence with top-notch cybersecurity measures, provide assistance with OWL Brightspace (our learning management system), and offer comprehensive technology help for everyone in the Western community.
