# In Memoriam

In This Section

Recognizing the passing of members of the campus community who during their careers shared their talents in helping Western students, faculty, staff and alumni to be their best.

Below you will find notices from 2025. For older notices (2012 to 2024) please visit our [In Memoriam archive page](memoriam_archive.html).

### 

### Flora Jean Fisher

Flora Jean Fisher, a retired staff member from the Faculty of Law passed away on November 6, 2025. Ms. Fisher retired in 1997 with 29 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.pattisonfuneralhome.com/obituary/Flora-Fisher)

### 

### Agron Alija

Dr. Agron Alija, an Adjunct Professor/MD from the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry, Department of Family Medicine passed away on October 21, 2025. Dr. Alija had a career of five years with Western at the time of his passing.

[Read obituary](https://www.arbormemorial.ca/en/forestlawn/obituaries/alija-agron-m-d/149720.html)

### 

### Jack Jung

Dr. Jack Jung, a retired faculty member from the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry - Department of Pediatrics passed away on July 24, 2025. Dr. Jung retired in 2013 after 31 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.westviewfuneralchapel.com/obituaries/dr-jack-howan-jung-m-d-f-r-c-p-c-a-b-m-g-f-c-c-m-g-professor-emeritus-of-western-university/)

### 

### Margorie Lanthiers

Margorie Lanthier, retired Vice-President (External) passed away September 3, 2025. During her term as V.P., she was known as Margorie Millar. Ms. Lanthier retired in 1994 after six years of service with Western.

### 

### Maurice Stubbs

Maurice Stubbs, retired University Art Curator with the McIntosh Gallery and part-time faculty member with the Department of Visual Arts passed away on August 2, 2025. Mr. Stubbs retired in 1989 after 20 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://lfpress.remembering.ca/obituary/maurice-stubbs-1093024161)

### 

### Adelheid Wahl

Adelheid (Heidy) Wahl, a retired staff member from Western Libraries passed away on September 4, 2025. Ms. Wahl retired in 1993 after 22 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://lfpress.remembering.ca/obituary/adelheid-wahl-1093071667?proxy=original)

### 

### Ann Haggerty

Ann Haggerty, a retired staff member from Office of the Registrar passed away on August 25, 2025. Ms. Haggerty retired in 1996 after 33 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.arbormemorial.ca/en/forestlawn/obituaries/ann-mary-haggerty/146781.html)

### 

### Seymour Ditor

Professor Emeritus Seymour Ditor, a faculty member from the Department of Mathematics passed away July 9, 2025. Dr. Ditor retired in 2004 after 30 years of full-time service with Western. Post-retirement, Dr. Ditor continued to teach part-time with the Department of Mathematics until 2016.

[Read obituary](https://www.remembering.ca/obituary/seymour-ditor-1092984743)

### 

### Frank Beier

Dr. Frank Beier, Chair and Professor with the Department of Physiology & Pharmacology passed away on July 18, 2025. Dr. Beier, an award-winning researcher, maintained a career of 24 years with Western, and was named Distinguished University Professor.

[Read obituary](https://harrisfuneralhome.ca/obituaries/frank-beier/)

### 

### Jodi Freeman

Jodi Freeman, Associate Director, Strategic Priorities with the Department of Housing & Ancillary Services has passed away on June 6, 2025. Ms. Freeman had a career of 15 years with the university, winning the Western Award of Excellence twice.

[Read obituary](https://everloved.com/life-of/jodi-freeman/obituary/)

### 

### Dan Gyetvai

Dan Gyetvai, Manager of the Electromechanical Shop in the Department of Facilities Management passed away on June 19, 2025. Mr. Gyetvai had a career of ten years with Western at the time of his passing.

[Read obituary](https://www.westviewfuneralchapel.com/obituaries/daniel-edward-gyetvai)

### 

### Alicja Sierkowski

Alicja Sierkowski, a retired staff member from the Department of Facilities Management passed away on June 14, 2025. Ms. Sierkowski retired in 2008 after 30 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.oneilfuneralhome.ca/obituaries/alicja-sierkowski)

### 

### Tim Hunt

Tim Hunt, a retired staff member from the Faculty of Engineering passed away on May 19, 2025. Mr. Hunt retired in 2016 after 30 years of service with Western..

[Read obituary](https://www.westviewfuneralchapel.com/obituaries/timothy-tim-joseph-hunt/)

### 

### Bruce Lundgren

Professor Emeritus Bruce Lundgren, a retired faculty member from the Department of English passed away on May 24, 2025. Dr. Lundgren retired in 2002 after 38 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://lfpress.remembering.ca/obituary/bruce-lundgren-1092899747)

### 

### Beth Hundey

Dr. Beth Hundey, passed away on April 23, 2025. Dr. Hundey was an Assistant Professor with the Department of Geography & Environment and an E-Learning and Curriculum Specialist with the Centre for Teaching and Learning during her 11-year career with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.westviewfuneralchapel.com/obituaries/elizabeth-beth-hundey/)

### 

### Ricardas Zitikis

Professor Ricardas Zitikis, a faculty member from the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science passed away on April 9, 2025. Professor Zitikis had a career of 24 years with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.northviewfuneralchapel.com/obituaries/ricardas-zitikis/)

### 

### Anna Evelyn Duff

Anna Evelyn Duff, a retired staff member from the Department of Hospitality Services at the Ivey Spencer Leadership Centre passed away on April 7, 2025. Ms. Duff retired in 2009 after 21 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.arbormemorial.ca/en/forestlawn/obituaries/anna-evelyn-duff/140076.html)

### 

### Richard Sorrentino

Professor Emeritus, Richard Sorrentino, a retired faculty member from the Department of Psychology passed away on March 22, 2025. Dr. Sorrentino retired in 2014 after 44 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://www.arbormemorial.ca/en/memorial/obituaries/richard-matthew-sorrentino/139223.html)

### 

### David Smith

Dr. David Smith, an Associate Professor and Medical Doctor with the Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry, Department of Anesthesia and Perioperative Medicine passed away on April 1, 2025. Dr. Smith’s career spanned 27 years with Western.

[Read obituary](https://logan-evans.ca/tribute/details/3955/David-Smith/obituary.html)

### 

### Roger Emerson

Professor Emeritus, Roger Emerson, a retired faculty member from the Department of History passed away on February 28, 2025. Dr. Emerson retired in 1990 after 35 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://harrisfuneralhome.ca/obituaries/roger-l-emerson/%20) [Department Tribute](https://history.uwo.ca/people/roger_l_emerson.html)

### 

### Mona Brennan-Coles

Mona Brennan-Coles, a retired staff member from the Department of Western Technology Services - Telecommunications Division, passed away on January 10, 2025. Ms. Brennan-Coles retired in 2020 after 30 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://cremationlondon.com/obituaries/mona-brennan-coles/)

### 

### Ann Drake

Ann Drake, a retired staff member from the School of Graduate and Post-Doctoral Studies passed away on January 8, 2025. Ms. Drake retired in 1997 after 17 years of service with Western.

[Read obituary](https://mcfarlane-roberts.ca/tribute/details/941/Ann-Drake/obituary.html)
