### Request Maintenance & Repairs

Complete this form for routine maintenance and repairs. For emergency requests, call Client Services (519-661-3304).

[[FM Maintenance & Repairs Requests](https://westernu.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/47/user/login?destination=portal%2F47%2Fgroup%2F214%2Fcreate%2F933)]

### Report Snow Hazards

Please report areas of campus which are slippery or inaccessible due to snow and ice. To report snow-related hazards outside of regular business hours, please call; **519-661-3304.**

[[Report a Hazard Form](what/snow_report.html "Complete a report of snow or icy conditions")]

### About Us

  
Facilities Management creates and maintains the built and natural environment at Western University. Our expert staff monitor and manage building performance, coordinate capital and departmental projects, conduct scheduled maintenance and cleaning requests, and respond to facilities related repairs and emergencies.  
([Learn More About Facilities Management](who/index.html))
