# Motorist Assistance Program

### Call us:

Mon - Fri between 8:30 am and 4:00 pm at **519-661-3973**

Mon - Fri after 4:00 pm, please call **519-661-3300** and [Western Special Constable Service](http://www.uwo.ca/police/) will assist you.**  
**

Parking & Visitor Services provides complimentary motorist assistance to all faculty, staff, students and visitors to Western University's main campus.

### We can help when...

  * Your car battery is dead and in need of a boost
  * You need your vehicle unlocked



**Please Note:** The Motorist Assistance Service may not be available at certain times throughout the day.
