# Faculty of Health Sciences Programs

The Faculty of Health Sciences offers a variety of undergraduate, graduate, professional and online programs designed to help students achieve their academic goals. Click on the program links below for more information.

#### [](school_based.html)

#### [**School-Based Undergraduate and Graduate Programs**](school_based.html)

Through its six schools, the Faculty of Health Sciences offers undergraduate, graduate and professional programs across a variety of academic disciplines that prepare students to shape the future of health and healthcare.

[Visit School-Based Programs](school_based.html "School-Based")

#### [](ahcp/index.html)

#### [**Advanced Health Care Practice** **(Graduate Program)**](ahcp/index.html)

The first program of its kind in Canada, this program offers experienced health-care professionals an opportunity to obtain graduate-level education in a clinical specialty related to their professional practice and become advanced practitioners.

[ Visit Advanced Health Care Practice](ahcp/index.html "AHCP")

#### [](ahs/index.html)

#### [**Applied Health Sciences****(Online Graduate Program)**](ahs/index.html)

This flexible, fully-online graduate diploma prepares learners to excel and work adaptively in this rapidly changing environment and contribute to the complex problem‐solving required by the health challenges of today and tomorrow.

[ Visit Applied Health Sciences](ahs/index.html "AHS")

#### [](hrs/index.html)

#### [**Health & Rehabilitation Sciences (Graduate Program)**](hrs/index.html)

The Graduate Program in Health & Rehabilitation Sciences offers both master's and PhD programs and combines knowledge bases that enable students to explore health-related issues and ideas in an interdisciplinary forum.

[Visit Health & Rehab Sciences](hrs/index.html)

#### [](school_based.html)

#### [**Health Information Science (Graduate Program)**](https://www.fims.uwo.ca/programs/graduate_programs/master_of_health_information_science/index.html)

Offered in partnership with the Faculty of Information and Media Studies, this research-based program sits at the intersection of healthcare and information management, policy, knowledge translation, and clinical practice.

[Visit Health Information Science](https://www.fims.uwo.ca/programs/graduate_programs/master_of_health_information_science/index.html "School-Based")

#### [](https://www.schulich.uwo.ca/global_health_systems/)

#### [**Global Health Systems (Graduate Program)**](https://www.schulich.uwo.ca/global_health_systems/)

This program provides students with organizational, leadership, and communication skills, enabling them to build successful careers in a growing and dynamic field of program management with a focus on global health.

[Visit Global Health Systems](https://www.schulich.uwo.ca/global_health_systems/)
