# Room Booking

Arts & Humanities rooms **UC 3105,****UC 3100,****UC TA 3100** can be booked through the Dean's Office.

Other rooms must be booked through the central Western bookings system: **[BOOK HERE](https://westernu.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1056/user/login?destination=portal%2F1056%2Fgroup%2F1267%2Fcreate%2F2169)**

### View Room Details

### Arts and Humanities Rooms - University College

**UC 3105 (Dean’s Boardroom, capacity: 25)**

**UC 3100 (James Good Room, capacity 47)**

**UC TA 3100 (James Good Ante Room, capacity 39)**

ACCESS TO ROOMS:

Access to these rooms is by key, available from the Dean’s Office – UC 2220.

Pick up and return: The Dean’s office is open 8:30-12:00 and 1:00-4:30 Monday to Friday.

Please return the key _as early as possible_ the next business day after the event.

_A late charge of $50 applies to keys not returned within one business day of the event._

Dean’s Boardroom Equipment (UC 3105):

  * HD Video Projector
  * Audio System
  * Windows Rack Computer
  * Network Connection
  * HDMI aux Input
  * Bluray Player



Technical Services: If you require technical services for your event in UC 3105, you must contact Arts and Humanities Technical Services at least three weeks in advance ([artstech@uwo.ca](mailto:artstech@uwo.ca))

### UC 3110 Conron Hall (Classroom, capacity: 178)

NOTE: Between 8:30am and 5:30pm Monday to Friday throughout the Academic year, Conron Hall is a general use classroom and is booked through [IPB](http://www.ipb.uwo.ca/reservations/). Otherwise, a request to book the room can be made through the Faculty of Arts and Humanities Dean’s office using the form below.

EQUIPMENT:

  * [video-data projector](https://wts.uwo.ca/ctg/documentation/videodata_projector.html)
  * document camera
  * [rack computer](https://wts.uwo.ca/ctg/documentation/rack_computer.html)(USB port, network connection, computer monitor)
  * VGA laptop connection
  * HDMI connection (cable not provided)
  * document camera
  * [sound system](https://wts.uwo.ca/ctg/documentation/videodata_projector.html)
  * [wireless microphone](https://wts.uwo.ca/ctg/documentation/wireless_microphone.html)



To access the equipment in this room you will need an [AV Key](https://wts.uwo.ca/ctg/documentation/keys.html).

### Complete the room booking by emailing **[Gillian Morrison](mailto:gmorri44@uwo.ca)**
