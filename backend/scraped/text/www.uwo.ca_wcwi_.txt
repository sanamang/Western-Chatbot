### About WCWI

Western's Caucus on Women’s Issues was formed in 1980 to promote and safeguard the interests of women at Western University and its affiliates. Our objectives are as follows:

  1. To cultivate a sense of community among women at the university.
  2. To encourage the integration of the findings from feminist research into curricula at Western and its affiliates.
  3. To promote a work environment which facilitates the full professional development of all women employed at Western and its affiliates.
