# Facilities Management Only - Service Vehicle Permits

### **FM Service Vehicle Parking Area Reconfiguration**

Please note that the Dental Circle and area behind (North side) Taylor Library have been reconfigured to reduce vehicle congestion.

Additional Service Vehicle Permit Parking has been added and is noted here in Orange. These are for Western Branded Service Vehicles only.
