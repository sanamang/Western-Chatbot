# Infractions

### [Pay Infraction](https://westernu.t2hosted.ca/Account/Portal)

Search by either your License Plate number or Citation/Transaction number and pay your citation online.

* * *

### [Appeal Infraction](appeals/index.html)

All parking infractions, with the exception of towing and service fees, may be appealed within fourteen days of the infraction date. Begin the appeal procedure now by completing the request form. [Learn More](appeals/index.html)

* * *

### [Academic Seals](academic_seals_and_sanctions.html)

Students who incur parking infractions which go unpaid may be subject to academic record seals. [Learn More](academic_seals_and_sanctions.html)

* * *

### [Towed Vehicles](towed_vehicles.html)

Unauthorized or improperly parked vehicles will be ticketed and towed at the owners' expense and liability. [Learn More](towed_vehicles.html)

* * *

### [Parking Regulations](regulations/index.html)

Purchase of any parking permit or day pass constitutes an agreement from the purchaser to abide to the University's Rules and Regulations. [Learn More](regulations/index.html)
