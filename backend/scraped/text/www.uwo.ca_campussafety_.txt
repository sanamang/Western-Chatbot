## News

  * **[New Campus 911 Signage Initiative](news/2025/New Campus 911 Signage Initiative.html)**  
September 25, 2025  
We are introducing a new Campus Safety & Emergency Services initiative to enhance emergency response. You will see the attached Building Emergency numbers posted in buildings throughout campus. The installation of the building emergency numbers will be completed by the end of 2025.
  * **[Coyotes sightings on Western campus](news/2024/recent_coyotes_sightings_on_western_campus.html)**  
March 28, 2024  
Western Special Constable Service has received several notifications from members of the campus community that have observed coyotes on campus property.

## What's Happening

  * **[WSCS Property Sale](whats-happening/2025/wscs_annual_property_sale.html)**  
May 13, 2025  
Western Special Constable Service (WSCS) is set to hold its unclaimed property sale on Thursday, May 22nd, 2025. The event will begin at 10:00am and conclude at 12:00pm or sooner if all items are purchased early. The sale will take place at the University Community Centre (UCC) atrium. 
  * **[Coyotes sightings on Western campus](whats-happening/2025/Building Emergency Response.html)**  
March 28, 2024  
Western Special Constable Service has received several notifications from members of the campus community that have observed coyotes on campus property.
