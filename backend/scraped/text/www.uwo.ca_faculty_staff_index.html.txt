### Living Well @ Western

Living Well @ Western provides free wellness activities that span the seven domains of wellness, intended to maintain and improve employee well-being. [Learn more](https://www.uwo.ca/hr/safety/well_being/wellness/living_well/index.html)

### Course Readings

Use this service to give your students free and easy access to course readings through Western Libraries. [Learn more](https://www.lib.uwo.ca/coursereadings/index.html?utm_source=external&utm_medium=web+banner&utm_id=Course+Readings+Fall+2023)

### 25 Year Service Awards

The 25-Year Service Award is one of the ways Western acknowledges and celebrates the contributions of long-serving faculty and staff members. [Learn more](https://www.uwo.ca/faculty_staff/25-year-service-awards/index.html)
