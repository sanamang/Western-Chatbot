# Discover our Viewbook

Are you ready to open up your curiosity and imagination? Bring your ideas and passion for science to Western.

Here, you join our community of scholars in high-tech collaborative workspaces and research labs. Build your own degree path with literally thousands of options. At Western, you are a scientist, taking your knowledge and know-how into the lab, into the field, into the workplace and around the world.

Science at Western is technical, challenging, creative and inspirational. We call it high-impact learning. You’ll call it highly rewarding.

Click through our digital Undergraduate Viewbook to discover the Western Science Experience and the many pathways you can combine to make your science education as unique as you.
