# Awards & Recognition

## [Teaching Awards of Excellence for Faculty](teaching_award.html "Supervi")

This award focuses on faculty who consistantly motivate students and are responsive to students' intellectual needs, or have developed innovative curricular materials. All full- and part-time faculty members of the Faculty of Health Sciences may be nominated.

[ Visit Teaching Awards ](teaching_award.html "Teaching Awards")

* * *

## [Award of Excellence in Graduate Student Supervision and Mentorship](supervision_mentorship_award.html "Award of Excellence in Graduate Supervision and Mentorship")

This award recognizes a faculty member in the Faculty of Health Sciences who has demonstrated a record of mentorship that fosters intellectual, professional, and personal development of graduate students in the Faculty of Health Sciences.

[ Visit Supervision and Mentorship Awards ](teaching_award.html "Teaching Awards")

* * *

## [Peer-to-Peer Staff Awards](staff_award.html "Staff Awards")

These awards highlight outstanding accomplishments, process innovations, and overall support within the faculty, university and broader health community. The awards have three categories: Impact, Stewardship and Leadership.

[ Visit Staff Awards ](staff_award.html "Staff Awards")

* * *

## [Outstanding Postdoctoral Scholar Award](postpoc_award.html "Outstanding Postdoctoral Scholar Awards")

Honoring postdoctoral scholars for exceptional commitment and potential, this award highlights those who excel in research and leadership. Their excellence not only in scholarship – but also in supervision, mentoring, and community service – sets them apart from their peers.

[ Visit Postdoctoral Awards ](/about/awards/postdoc_award "Western Awards")

* * *

## [Western Awards](western_awards.html "Western Awards")

To recognize its most outstanding teachers, researchers and professors, Western University supports awards and honours designed to celebrate those who display a commitment to excellence. 

[ Visit Western Awards ](western_awards.html "Western Awards")

* * *

## [Alumni Awards & Recognition](alumni_awards.html "Alumni Awards & Recognition")

The Faculty of Health Sciences is committed to celebrating the accomplishments of its members, including alumni.These alumni enter a diverse range of professions, achieving excellence, driving innovation and promoting health for all people.

[ Visit Alumni Awards & Recognition ](alumni_awards.html "Honor List")
