# Diversity

In This Section

Western’s equity, diversity, inclusion and decolonization (EDID) approach aims to combat racism, discrimination and ableism in all their manifestations. We strive to cultivate a campus environment where all community members can fully engage in university life, learning, working, contributing, thriving and feeling safe.
