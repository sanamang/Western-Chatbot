# Electric Vehicle Charging Stations

Western currently offers several EV Charging Stations on campus. See the [parking map](https://www.uwo.ca/parking/find/map.html) for locations. 

The [ChargePoint](https://www.chargepoint.com/en-ca/) app will show which of the stations are currently active for use.

**As of July 1, 2025, the following fee structure for EV charging are:**  
**Level 2: $3.00/hour**  
**Level 3: $20.00/hour**

The above rates do not include any connection fees charged independently by Chargepoint.

Charging Stations can be found at the following locations:

  * Alumni Thompson
  * Bayfield
  * Brescia
  * Elborn
  * Health Science Pay and Display
  * Huron Flats
  * Ontario Hall
  * South Valley
  * Springett Pay and Display
  * Support Services
  * Weldon
  * Westminster
  * Visual Arts



EV Charging Station Policies and Procedures:

  * Only electric vehicles that are plugged into a charging station are permitted to park in spaces designated with an EV parking sign.
  * Non-Active Western Parking Permit Holders must pay for visitor parking while charging. Visitor parking times and rates can be found at the following link: [Visitor Parking](https://www.uwo.ca/parking/find/visitor/index.html).
  * Tickets will be issued for: Parking a non-electric vehicle in a designated EV charging space, and parking an electric vehicle in a designated EV charging space without it being plugged in.
  * Electric vehicles must be actively charging while occupying the space. Vehicles must be moved when charging is complete.
  * The EV charging station spaces cannot be used for loading and unloading, temporary parking or as a pick-up or drop-off area.

  


If you have questions about these stations or require access, please contact Parking Services at wparking@uwo.ca.
