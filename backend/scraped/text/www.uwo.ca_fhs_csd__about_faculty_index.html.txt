# Research Supervisors

## [](aud_supervisors.html)Audiology

Audiology researchers in the School of Communication Sciences and Disorders explore the science of hearing from a variety of perspectives and throughout the lifespan. The National Centre for Audiology serves as a hub for research in this area and brings together expertise from around the world.

[ Meet the Researchers](aud_supervisors.html "Audiology Supervisors")

## Speech-Language Pathology

Studying speech and language science in a number of contexts, researchers in the School of Communication Sciences and Disorders are making discoveries that help enhance the wellness of people in all stages of life and allow people to be more engaged with the world around them. 

[ Meet the Researchers](slp_supervisors.html "Speech-Language Pathology Supervisors")
