# Combined HBA Degree

This five-year program combining a BA Honours with an HBA from the Ivey Business School is a unique blend of a strong arts foundation and world-class business education preparing graduates to become leaders in a wide array of industries, including entertainment, publishing, art, travel, fashion, tech, finance, and many more. 

The combined program leads to an HBA and a BA Honors in one of the Honours Specialization modules offered in the Faculty of Arts and Humanities:

  * Art History and Museum Studies
  * Art History and Studio Art
  * Classical Studies 
  * Creative Writing and English Language and Literature
  * English Language and Literature
  * Film Studies
  * French Studies
  * Global Gender Studies
  * Linguistics
  * Philosophy
  * Spanish Language and Hispanic Cultures
  * Women's Studies



In Years 1 and 2, students are registered in the Faculty of Arts and Humanities and follow the normal curriculum for their Honours Specialization module including Business 2257.

In Year 3, students are registered in the HBA program. Students are registered in the combined program in Years 4 and 5.

For further information on admission and progression requirements as well as program requirements: Visit to [HBA/Arts](http://www.westerncalendar.uwo.ca/Departments.cfm?DepartmentID=140&SelectedCalendar=Live&ArchiveID "Go to Western's Academic Calendar for HBA/Arts Dual Degree")
