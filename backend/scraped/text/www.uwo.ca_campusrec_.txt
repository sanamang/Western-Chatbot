### Intramural 1-day Fall Tournaments - Register Now

Western Intramural 1-Day Tournaments are back for the Fall 2025 term! Spots are filling up fast, so register now. These short format sport tournaments take place in late November. Tournament sports offered: Badminton, Pickleball, Dodgeball, Volleyball, Basketball, and Inner-Tube Water Polo.[ (Read full entry)](news/2025/intramural_1day_fall_tournaments__register_now.html)

### Move Through the Mess: Finding Strength in Imperfect Moments

When life feels messy, it’s easy to put wellness on the back burner. Assignments pile up, exams approach, and stress levels rise. But this month’s Wellness Wednesday theme reminds us that movement doesn’t need to be perfect to matter. Sometimes, the most important thing you can do is simply keep moving—through the mess.[ (Read full entry)](news/2025/move_through_the_mess_finding_strength_in_imperfect_moments.html)

**October 1, 2025  
**

### Do Something You Like: Finding Joy in Movement 

When it comes to staying active, the best workout is the one you actually enjoy. Research shows that when people choose activities they find fun, they are more likely to stick with them long-term (CDC, 2022). That’s why this October, Campus Recreation’s Wellness Wednesday series focuses on one simple theme: Do Something You Like. Movement doesn’t have to mean running on a treadmill or lifting weights. It can be dancing, rollerblading, playing frisbee with friends, or taking a walk between classes. What matters most isn’t what you do—it’s how much you enjoy it. [ (Read full entry)](news/2025/do_something_you_like_finding_joy_in_movement.html)

###  Lifesaving Society -Standard First Aid & CPR C/AED

This Lifesaving Society course provides comprehensive training covering all aspects of first aid,CPR and Automated External Defibrillation (AED). It is designed for those who require a more in-depth understanding of first aid and is recognized by the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) and also satisfies the first aid requirements in the Ontario Public Pools Regulation. Dates options are available throughout the Fall.[ (Read full entry)](news/2025/_lifesaving_society_standard_first_aid__cpr_caed.html)
