# Your Benefits

Benefits are an important part of your total compensation package at Western. For details on the benefits that are available to you, please begin by choosing your Employee Group. You will then be able to view a summary of your benefits, access your full benefit booklet and see your employee group's collective agreement and/or appropriate policies.

If you do not know your employee group, consult with your supervisor or [use **ASK HR** to contact Human Resources](../../contact.html). Some part-time and/or temporary positions are not represented under any of the groups listed below.

  * [Full-time Faculty Members](faculty.html "Faculty")
  * [Clinical Teachers Association Members](clinical_faculty/index.html "Clinical Faculty")
  * [Professional Managerial Association](professionals_managers.html "Professionals & Managers")
  * [Staff Association Members](uwosa/index.html)
  * [SAGE Employees ](staff_union_exempt.html "Staff Union Exempt")
  * [CUPE Facilities Management Members](cupe2361/index.html "Facilities Management Union")



  * [CUPE Hospitality Services Members](cupe2692/index.html "Hospitality Services Union")
  * [Librarians & Archivists](librarians_archivists.html "Librarians & Archivists")
  * [Western Special Constable Service Members](wscs_opseu.html "WSCS")
  * [Operating Engineers](operating_engineers.html "Operating Engineers")
  * [Postdoctoral Associates](pda/index.html "Postdoctoral Associates")
  * [Graduate Teaching Assistants](gta_benefits.html)
