All of our Recreational Sport Clubs are recreational and physically active or athletic in nature. Some Clubs may offer additional features such as instructional classes for skill development and competitive opportunities for those who chose to participate.

LEGEND:

* - New club as of the current year.

R - This Club is entirely recreational. May or may not have internal competitions within the Club.

I - This Club is recreational in nature with an instructional component, meaning an instructor leads classes for skill development.

C - This Club is recreational in nature but also offers competitive opportunities for Club members to participate in open tournaments or against other universities. 

Recreational Sport Clubs Schedule (2025-2026)

SPORT CLUBS WEEK
September 15-19 in the WSRC Atrium

 

  • Sunday, September 21st - Start of Sport Clubs Season
  • Monday, October 13th - Thanksgiving Monday (no sessions)
  • Sunday, November 2nd to Saturday, November 8th (inclusive) - Fall Reading Week Break
  • Saturday, December 6th - Last day of sessions for the Fall Term

WINTER HOLIDAY BREAK

  • Sunday, January 18th - Beginning of Winter Term; Sport Clubs sessions resume
  • Sunday, February 15th to Saturday, February 21st (inclusive) - Winter Reading Week Break
  • Friday, April 3rd - Good Friday (no sessions)
  • Saturday, April 4th - End of Sport Clubs Season
