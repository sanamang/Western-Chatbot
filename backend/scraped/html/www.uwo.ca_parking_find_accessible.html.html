Accessible Parking on Campus

Accessible Western Parking Permit

Members of the University Community with long term or temporary physical disabilities may apply for access to all areas designated with the recognized international symbol. Rates for an Accessible Western Parking Permit are equivalent to the Perimeter Non-Reserved fees specific to your status on campus. If you require a Western Accessible Permit, please send an email to wparking@uwo.ca and we'll get you set up with the appropriate permit. 

Where can I park if I don't have an Accessible Western Parking Permit?

Visitors who require accessible parking are granted access to all Pay and Display and Honk Mobile zones on campus. Please note, parking must be paid when displaying an Ontario Ministry of Transportation Accessible Parking Permit.

Accessibility Map

Western’s Campus Accessibility Map may be of assistance in locating accessible routes between buildings on campus.
