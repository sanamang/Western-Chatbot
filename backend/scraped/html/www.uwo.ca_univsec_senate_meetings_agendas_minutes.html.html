Agendas and Minutes

Senate Meeting Agendas and Approved Minutes

The Senate Agenda - including the Minutes of the previous meeting - is normally posted a week in advance of the meetings. Occasionally, changes to the Agenda/Minute package need to be made between the original date of posting and the date of the Senate meeting. Highlight and strikethrough will be used to indicate these changes and will be removed once the impacted meeting is adjourned.
