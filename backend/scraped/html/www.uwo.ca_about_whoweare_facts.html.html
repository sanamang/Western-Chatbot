Facts & Figures

2024–2025

  • Founded: 1878
  • President: Alan Shepard
  • Total Revenue: $1.67 billion
  • Endowment: $1.3 billion
  • Annual Research Funding: $283.74 million
  • Library Book Holdings: 6.11 million
  • Land Area: 495.7 hectares

Key Facts

  • World-ranked – Western is ranked among the top 1% of higher education institutions worldwide
  • Research-intensive – We are a founding member of the U15, Canada’s group of most distinguished research universities
  • Global campus – Our 42,978 students include 5,445 international students from 134 countries
  • Top students – One of Canada’s top entrance averages for first-year students at 90.9%*
  • High retention rate – One of Canada’s highest first to second year retention rate at 92.6%*
  • Scholarships, Awards, Fellowships, Teaching Assistantships and Financial Aid – More than $100 million provided annually
  • Research impact – Western researchers published 5,960 papers in 2024
  • Prestigious scholars – Western has had 24 Rhodes Scholars, 85 Fellows of the Royal Society of Canada, and has 70 active Canada Research Chairs
  • Comprehensive academics – 12 faculties including professional programs in medicine, business, law, engineering and the School of Graduate & Postdoctoral Studies
  • Tailored programs – 400+ combinations of undergraduate majors, minors and specializations
  • Diverse graduate offerings – 160 graduate degree programs and collaborative specializations, including a range of interdisciplinary programs
  • Solid career outcomes – 93.5% employment rate six months after graduation
  • International reach – More than 382,000 alumni in 160 countries worldwide

* 2023-24 data.
