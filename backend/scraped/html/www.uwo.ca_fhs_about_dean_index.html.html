Office of the Dean

The Dean's Office team provides leadership and support to faculty, staff and students across all areas of the Faculty of Health Sciences and the broader Western community. 

Meet the Dean

Shaun Boe joined Western University as Dean of the Faculty of Health Sciences in 2025. A professor of Physical Therapy, he conducts research in neuroscience and rehabilitation, with a focus on entrepreneurship and commercialization in neurotechnology.

Senior Administration

The Faculty of Health Sciences is guided by a dedicated and talented group of leaders who bring expertise from a variety of disciplines and backgrounds.

 

Dean's Office Staff

Staff members in the Office of the Dean provide a variety of critical supports to members across all areas of the Faculty of Health Sciences.
