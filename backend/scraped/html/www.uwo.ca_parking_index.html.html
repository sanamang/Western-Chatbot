Fall/Annual Term Student Parking Permits - SOLD OUT

The Fall 2025 (September 1st to December 31st) and Annual 2025/26 (September 1st 2025 to August 31st 2026) Student Parking Permits are sold out as of July 4 2025.

The next available Student Parking Permits which will be available for sale will be for the Winter Term (Jan 2026 – Apr 2026) and are scheduled to be available for sale on November 15 2025.

We will be running a waitlist for the Annual and Fall Term Student Parking Permits and movement on the waitlist will directly depend on how many of the Annual and Fall Term Student Parking Permits which were sold are returned to the parking office.  

If you would like to be added to the waitlist, please select the following link.

Winter Term Student Parking Permits - Available November 15 2025

 The Winter Term (Jan 1 2026 - April 31 2026) Student Parking Permits will be available for sale online on November the 15th at midnight (12:00am).  Limited quantities will be availabe for purchase and they will be sold on a first come first serve basis.

Service Interruption - Alumni Thompson Visitor Parking Lot (North Section) and South Valley Reserved Spaces Relocation (North Section) Sept 23 2024 - Aug 31 2026.

The Alumni Thompson visitor parking lots northern section will be closed to support construction of the new engineering building.  The southern section of the Alumni Thompson visitor parking lot will remain open for visitor parking and payment for visitor parking can be made through Honkmobile.

The South Valley reserved parking spaces along the north section of the South Valley parking lot will be closed to support construction of the new engineering building.  Additional reserved spaces will be added to the south section of the South Valley parking lot.
