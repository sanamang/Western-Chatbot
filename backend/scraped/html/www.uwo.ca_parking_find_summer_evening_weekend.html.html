Summer, Evening & Weekend Parking Options

Evening Parking

  • Complimentary parking is available in the evenings from 4:00 pm - 7:00 am in the Elborn Lot and from 7:00 pm - 7:00 am in the Visual Arts and Upper Heating lots (please do not park in metered or reserved spaces).
  • Parking is available in most gated, unattended lots between 6:00 pm - 6:00 am at a rate of $1.50 per half hour to a maximum of $7.00 - payment by credit card on exit.

Weekend Parking Options

Complimentary Parking

Friday at 8:00 pm to Monday at 6:00 am complimentary parking is available at:

  • Alumni Thompson Visitor Parking lot (excluding event days)

Friday at 7:00 pm to Monday at 6:00 am complimentary parking is available at:

  • Upper Heating
  • Visual Arts
  • Siebens

Friday at 5:00 pm to Monday at 6:00 am complimentary parking is available at:

  • Springett Student
  • Springett Staff
  • Medway lot behind Elgin Hall Residence
  • Huron Flats

Friday at 4:00 pm to Monday at 6:00 am complimentary parking is available at:

  • Elborn College - regular lot spaces
  • Talbot Staff - gated lot

 

Gated Lots - pay by credit card

Social Science Visitor lot is available for parking at in the non-reserved spaces.  Payment is made at the exit by credit card.  The rate is $1.50 per half hour up to the two hour mark.  After two hours, the daily maximum rate of $21.00 is charged.

*Please note that some lots will be unavailable at times due to special events or closed for the winter season.

Honk Mobile (No Maximum Rate)

Located at various buildings in the campus core and where ever there is Pay & Display parking.

Rates are as posted ($1.50 per half hour) 24 hours a day and 7 days a week. *NOTE EXCEPTION:  no parking at Support Services (Lot K) Honk spaces from 8 pm - 7 am. 

Pay & Display (Multi-space Meter Parking)

Located at:

  • Brescia Circle
  • Essex/London Hall
  • Faculty of Education
  • Labatt Health Science Building
  • Medical Science Bldg.
  • Music Bldg.
  • North Campus Bldg.
  • Ontario Hall
  • Support Services Building
  • Talbot College
  • Westminster Hall

Rate: $1.50 per half hour - 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Credit Card minimum: $1.50 (Visa, Visa Debit, Mastercard and American Express are accepted).

 

Summer Parking

Parking access on campus remains the same during the summer months. Please review the individual lot details for information.

The parking office also has a student summer term pass available. This permit allows students to park in a variety of lots on campus including all day (24hrs/day, 7 days/week) access to:

  • Althouse Student Lot
  • West Campus Lot
  • Huron Flats Lot
  • Westminster
  • Medway Lot
  • Springett Student Lot
  • South Valley
    (after 9:30am)

 

 The Student Summer permit also allows limited access (after 4:00 pm, Mon-Fri) to some campus core lots. See the Parking map for lot locations or contact the Parking Office for additional information.
