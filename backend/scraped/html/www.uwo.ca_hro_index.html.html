Human Rights Office

Welcome

The Human Rights Office supports Western’s respectful and inclusive working and learning environment by providing advice and guidance on Western’s Non-Discrimination, Harassment and Sexual Misconduct Policy. We are dedicated to making Western a safe, respectful and supportive environment for all members of the university community.

How We Can Help You

Report a Concern

The Western Human Rights Office (HRO) is here to help. We provide advice and support to all members of Western's campus community: students, staff, faculty, and leaders.
