Towed Vehicles

Unauthorized or improperly parked vehicles will be ticketed and may be towed at the owners' expense and liability. Park only in marked parking areas. Do not park in loading zones, roadways, fire routes, aisle ways, landscaped areas or other non-parking areas.

If you suspect that your car has been towed, please contact the Parking Office or Western Special Constable Service.

All towing of vehicles at Western University is performed by a private contractor in accordance with City of London standards. Please be advised that towing fees and applicable taxes are subject to change without notice.

Procedure Fee
Towing (service charge) $160.00* plus HST

* Towing Fee may be subject to fee increase due to method of towing and distance of the tow.
