Employee Groups & Collective Agreements

Western has a diverse workforce.  The following is a list of employee groups at Western. Some are unions, and some are associations. Choose a group to see more details and find links to further information.

  • CUPE Facilities Management
  • CUPE Hospitality Services
  • Clinical Teachers Association
  • Faculty Association
  • Graduate Teaching Assistants
  • Librarians & Archivists
  • Operating Engineers
  • Western Special Constable Service
  • Postdoctoral Associates
  • ProfessionalManagerialAssociation
  • SAGE
  • UWOSA

CUPE Facilities Management (Canadian Union of Public Employees Local 2361)

CUPE represents employees in Facilities Management who are engaged in the maintenance and service of buildings and grounds at the University.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2024
  • View updated salary information (Western login required)
  • Approximate number: 360
  • CUPE 2361 website

CUPE Hospitality Services (Canadian Union of Public Employees Local 2692)

CUPE Hospitality Services represents the employees in the Hospitality Services Department at Western.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026
  • Approximate number: 570
  • CUPE 2692 website

Clinical Teachers Association of UWO (CTA – UWO)

The CTA-UWO represents Clinical Academic Faculty with full-time appointments in the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry.
  • Approximate number: 750
  • Current Memorandum of Agreement – July 1, 2020 – June 30, 2026

Faculty Association (UWOFA)

The University of Western Ontario Faculty Association represents full-time professors of the University (other than clinical faculty) and also part-time faculty with duties equivalent to teaching at least 0.5 degree credit course in each of 2 of the last 3 fiscal years.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2022 to June 30, 2026
  • Approximate number: 1571
  • UWOFA website

Graduate Teaching Assistants (PSAC Local 610)

PSAC Local 610 represents registered graduate students who are engaged in supervised teaching-related activities across the various Faculties and Schools.
  • See the current collective agreement - September 1, 2023 to August 31, 2027
  • Approximate number: 1950
  • PSAC Local 610 website

Librarians & Archivists (UWOFA-LA)

UWOFA-LA represents those employees of the University performing the work of librarians and archivists.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2027
  • Approximate number: 50
  • UWOFA website

Operating Engineers (IUOE Local 772)

IUOE represents the University's Operating Engineers and those individuals engaged primarily as their helpers in the main Power Plant and other remote plants.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2027
  • Approximate number: 13

Western Special Constable Service (OPSEU Local 102)

OPSEU Local 102 represents employees in the Western Special Constable Service.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2023
  • Approximate number: 9

Postdoctoral Associates (PSAC Local 610 - Unit 2; formerly Local 611)

PSAC represents Postdoctoral Associates at the University, who are engaged in research and are employees of the University.
  • See the current collective agreement - January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2025
  • Approximate number: 230
  • PSAC Local 610 website

Professional Managerial Association (PMA)

The PMA represents full-time employees engaged on a permanent basis in managerial and professional roles across the University.
  • Current Memorandum of Agreement - Nov. 1, 2022 to Oct. 31, 2025
  • See PMA Policies
  • Approximate number: 1290
  • PMA website

SAGE - Select Administrative Group Employees

SAGE represents University employees engaged in administrative roles that may require access to confidential employment and labour relations information, necessitating their exclusion from the Staff Association bargaining unit.
  • Approximate number: 7
  • See SAGE policies

UWOSA - Staff Association

UWOSA represents employees engaged in administrative and research support roles employed on a continuing or sessional (e.g. academic year) basis.  Employees hired temporarily in these roles for eight months or more and working regularly more than 24 hours per week are also represented by UWOSA.
  • See the current collective agreement - July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2028
  • View updated salary information (Western login required)
  • Approximate number: 885
  • UWOSA website
  • View current classifications and more information on the Job Evaluation System (JES) transition (login required)
  • View UWOSA Successful Candidate list
