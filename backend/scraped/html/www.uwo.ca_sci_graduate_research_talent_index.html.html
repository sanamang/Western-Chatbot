Research Talent

Through experienced-based research, Western Science’s graduate students are trained to utilize innovative techniques and technologies to solve complex scientific problems. This research intensive environment develops and produces top caliber researchers who are recognized by numerous prestigious awards and go on to distinguished careers in both academia and industry. We take this opportunity to introduce you to a selection of our graduate student cohort.
