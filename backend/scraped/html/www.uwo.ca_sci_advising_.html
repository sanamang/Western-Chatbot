Notices

IMPORTANT DATES:

September 4: First day of classes

September 8: Deadline for applications for graduation: Autumn Convocation

September 12: Last day to add or drop a fall 12-week course or fall/winter 24 week course. 

September 30: National Day for Truth & Reconciliation (no classes)

November 3-9: Fall Reading Week

December 1: Last to day to withdraw from a fall 12-week course (resulting in a grade of WDN)

December 9: Fall term classes end

December 10: Study day

December 11-22: Final exams

*More Important Dates can be found in the Western Academic Calendar here.
