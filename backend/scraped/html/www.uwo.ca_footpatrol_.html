Who We Are & Hours

Western Foot Patrol is a volunteer driven service that provides safe walks on the Western, Huron and King's college campuses, along with some off campus locations. We patrol campus, reporting anything of concern; and conduct emergency blue phone and campus lighting audits.

November Hours

November 10, 2025 - We are open for safewalks Monday-Thursday 7pm-12am; Friday 8-11pm. Lost & Found is open Monday-Friday 12-4pm.

News & Events

Volunteer Applications 2025/2026

September 8, 2025 -  Volunteer applications are now available for the 2025/26 school year. Applicatons are located at the top of the page under the volunteer tab. Completed applications can be emailed to wfp@uwo.ca.

Plain Clothes Walks

September 8, 2025 - We will once again be offering Plain Clothes Walks! This means we can walk you to your destination without our identifiable vests, so it looks like you're walking with friends. Just let us know when you call or text via our app that you would like a Plain Clothes Walk!

 

App Decomissioned

September 8, 2025 - Foot Patrol has decomissioned the app at this time. The vast majority of calls for service came through calling the office or in person. We could consider something similar in the future if there is increased demand.
