Technical Support

Services we offer include computer hardware and software installation, and computer/peripheral maintenance and repair.

Computing Services are only available to the faculty and staff of the Faculty of Arts and Humanities. 

Please contact artstech@uwo.ca with any questions or support enquiries.

Smart Classrooms

Also called electronic or technology enhanced classrooms, these rooms are equipped with ceiling-mounted data projectors, computers and BluRay players. Laptop connectivity is available, as is internet connectivity (through the use of the rack-mounted computer or wireless connection through WiFi).

Inquiries should be directed depending on what you require:

  • Booking of smart classrooms is handled by your departmental office.
  • Instruction on the use of smart classrooms is handled by Technical Services.
  • Instructional Teaching Spaces that we support
  • All other teaching spaces not listed above: Classroom Technology Group
  • Classroom Physical Conditions: Facilities Management
    • Phone: (519) 661-2111 ext.83304
    • Email: fm-help@uwo.ca
