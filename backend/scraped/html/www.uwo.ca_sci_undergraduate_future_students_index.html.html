Are you ready to take the next big step in your academic career? It is time to choose where you will spend the next four years; with whom you will create life-long relationships; what you will learn and how you will prepare to take on the challenges and opportunities adult life will offer.

Western Science is the right place for you if you want to surround yourself with the top-performing students in Ontario; if you seek the freedom to craft your degree path; if you desire access to a wide variety of   high-impact   activities, courses, and programs that will challenge and equip you with technical expertise and professional competencies. The Faculty of Science at Western University is one of Canada’s finest institutions of advanced learning. Our diverse community is comprised of nearly 6,000 undergraduate students taught by faculty across eight departments.

The Western undergraduate academic experience is unique in Canada, offering each student the opportunity to design a custom course of study that aligns best with their career aspirations. Students can choose from approximately 140 modules and 480 courses in Science and Basic Medical Sciences, combine modules across departments and faculties, and pursue dual degrees with professional schools, including Engineering and one of the finest business schools in the world.

Discover Modular Degree Structures at Western Science

Western's modular system allows you to combine a Science module with a module in another science discipline to tailor your degree to fit your interests and aspirations. The department determines the specific courses included in each module. You have thousands of options.

Health, Wellness, and Advising

From the time you accept your letter of offer to the moment you graduate, Western Science’s academic support services work with you to ensure that your academic potential is fulfilled. 

Whether it’s registering for the courses that align best with your interests, maintaining mental health and wellness, or ensuring progress toward your degree,  Academic Advising  is your academic advocate.
