Procurement Post

Procurement Services’ mission is to provide support to the Western community and one way we do this is by listening to you! The Procurement Post (P-Post) is a newsletter full of tips, helpful information and upcoming events which have been compiled based on;

  • campus feedback
  • changes that may impact the purchasing roles in the departments and faculties and
  • new vendor contracts or projects implemented by Procurement.

If you have a topic or suggestion for a future issue of the P-Post, please email procurement@uwo.ca.
