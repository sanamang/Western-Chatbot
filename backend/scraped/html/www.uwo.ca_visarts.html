  • AJE24 | artLAB Gallery

    The Annual Juried Exhibition returns for its 24th consecutive year! This highly anticipated group exhibition represents a diverse selection of artwork from all disciplines and all levels of undergraduate study in the Department of Visual Arts. Exhibited works were selected by a professional jury who consider creativity, concept, materiality and technique. Please join us to celebrate our talented students! Exhibition: November 13 – December 4, 2025 Opening Reception: November 13 | 6:00pm – 8:00pm People’s Choice voting: 6:00 - 7:00pm AJE Award Announcements: 7:15pm
