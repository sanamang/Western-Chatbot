Welcome to Western Science. As the second-largest Faculty on campus, with seven departments and a growing portfolio of centres and facilities that serve our more than 6,000 students and several hundred researchers and instructors, we have a great deal of information to share and services to offer. While we invite you to take the time to explore the Faculty of Science website, we are sensitive to the need for quick overviews and contacts, which you will find here, to help you reach the right person or gain access to correct information as efficiently as possible.
