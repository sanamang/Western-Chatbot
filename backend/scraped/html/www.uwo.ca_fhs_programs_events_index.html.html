Events for Future Students

For prospective undergraduate and graduate students looking to explore their options for the next steps of their academic careers, a variety of events are held each year to connect you with the program that is right for you.

Fall Preview Day



Meet faculty, students and staff from across campus and discover Western's more than 400 undergraduate specializations, majors and minors.

March Break Open House



Discover Canada's best student experience, take a campus tour and get in-depth information about the undergraduate programs that interest you.

Ontario Universities Fair



Take the first step in your postsecondary journey and meet with representatives from each of Ontario's 21 universities - all under one roof. 

Professional Program Preview Day



Students looking to take the next step in their academic journey can learn about the professional programs offered by Western's Faculty of Health Sciences.
