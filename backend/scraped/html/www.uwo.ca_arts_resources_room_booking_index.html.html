Room Booking

Arts & Humanities rooms UC 3105, UC 3100, UC TA 3100 can be booked through the Dean's Office.

Other rooms must be booked through the central Western bookings system: BOOK HERE

View Room Details

Arts and Humanities Rooms - University College

UC 3105 (Dean’s Boardroom, capacity: 25)

UC 3100 (James Good Room, capacity 47)

UC TA 3100 (James Good Ante Room, capacity 39)

ACCESS TO ROOMS:

Access to these rooms is by key, available from the Dean’s Office – UC 2220.

Pick up and return: The Dean’s office is open 8:30-12:00 and 1:00-4:30 Monday to Friday.

Please return the key as early as possible the next business day after the event.

A late charge of $50 applies to keys not returned within one business day of the event. 

Dean’s Boardroom Equipment (UC 3105):

  • HD Video Projector
  • Audio System
  • Windows Rack Computer
  • Network Connection
  • HDMI aux Input
  • Bluray Player

Technical Services: If you require technical services for your event in UC 3105, you must contact Arts and Humanities Technical Services at least three weeks in advance (artstech@uwo.ca)

UC 3110 Conron Hall (Classroom, capacity: 178)

NOTE: Between 8:30am and 5:30pm Monday to Friday throughout the Academic year, Conron Hall is a general use classroom and is booked through IPB. Otherwise, a request to book the room can be made through the Faculty of Arts and Humanities Dean’s office using the form below.

EQUIPMENT:

  • video-data projector
  • document camera
  • rack computer(USB port, network connection, computer monitor)
  • VGA laptop connection
  • HDMI connection (cable not provided)
  • document camera
  • sound system
  • wireless microphone

To access the equipment in this room you will need an AV Key.

Complete the room booking by emailing Gillian Morrison
